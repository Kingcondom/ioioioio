### [MOCK-001] ข้อ 1 · The most appropriate management plan
Lecture: M01 Section 1 · Infectious disease · NL Part 1 · ชุดจำลอง

Q: A 24-year-old man presents with a 4-day history of high fever, severe retro-orbital headache, myalgias, and widespread body rash. On examination, he is febrile at 38.8°C, blood pressure is 110/70 mmHg, and pulse rate is 88/min. Laboratory investigation shows Hematocrit 44%, WBC 3,100/µL, and Platelet count 25,000/µL. There is no active mucosal or gastrointestinal bleeding. Which of the following is the most appropriate management plan?

A. Administer prophylactic intravenous ceftriaxone
B. Perform urgent bone marrow aspiration and biopsy
C. Administer high-dose intravenous methylprednisolone
D. Initiate intravenous fluid hydration and monitor serial hematocrit and clinical signs
E. Transfuse 10 units of platelet concentrate immediately

ANSWER: D. Initiate intravenous fluid hydration and monitor serial hematocrit and clinical signs

EXPLAIN:
ผู้ป่วยมีอาการเข้าได้กับ Dengue fever (ไข้เลือดออกเดงกี) ที่ไม่มีการเลือดออกรุนแรง การดูแลรักษาที่ถูกต้องคือการให้สารน้ำทดแทน (IV
fluids) และติดตาม serial hematocrit และสัญญาณชีพอย่างใกล้ชิด ไม่มีความจำเป็นต้องให้เกล็ดเลือดหากไม่มี active bleeding รุนแรง

### [MOCK-002] ข้อ 2 · The drug of choice for this patient

Chest radiograph demonstrates a right upper lobe cavitary infiltrate. Gram stain of the sputum shows weakly Gram-positive, branching filamentous rods. Vital signs reveal blood pressure 110/70 mmHg, pulse 104/min, respiratory rate 26/min, and SpO2 86% on room air. Chest radiograph reveals bilateral diffuse symmetrical ground-glass opacities. ### [MOCK-007] ข้อ 7 · The recommended induction therapy

Q: A 42-year-old man with advanced HIV infection presents with severe headache, fever, and neck stiffness. Physical examination reveals positive Kernig and Brudzinski signs. ### [MOCK-008] ข้อ 8 · The most appropriate management for the sexual partner
Lecture: M01 Section 1 · Infectious disease · NL Part 1 · ชุดจำลอง

Q: A 30-year-old individual is diagnosed with early latent syphilis. The patient reports having had a single sexual partner within the past 60 days. The partner is currently asymptomatic and nonreactive on RPR screening. Which of the following is the most appropriate management for the sexual partner?

A. Reassure the partner that no treatment or follow-up is necessary
B. Recheck RPR blood test in 3 months before deciding on treatment
C. Perform darkfield microscopy on urethral/vaginal swab
D. Administer Benzathine Penicillin G 2.4 million units IM as a single dose immediately

### [MOCK-010] ข้อ 10 · The primary step in management
Lecture: M01 Section 1 · Infectious disease · NL Part 1 · ชุดจำลอง

Q: A 35-year-old flood-relief worker presents with 10 episodes of profuse, painless, watery diarrhea resembling 'rice water' accompanied by severe muscle cramps and vomiting.
