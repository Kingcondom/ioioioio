## p10–11 IVP/IVU (ภาพ · Madaan PK, Ghasi RG. Br J Radiol 2022;95:20210949)

## p12 IVP — ไตปกติ (Krairittichai U, Leehacharoenkul S, Dowreang J. J Med Assoc Thai 2011;94:S23-8)
- Location: **Right L1–L3 · Left T12–L2**
- Length: **9.3–10.7 cm (Thai)** · **9–12 cm (international)**
- Outline/contour · **Axis parallel to psoas**

## p13–14 IVP "AFTER 15 MIN" (ภาพ)
- **Interpapillary line should run parallel to renal outline**
- ภาพเทียบ Normal vs Hydronephrosis (มองไม่เห็น)

## p15 IVP — เวลาหลังฉีดสารทึบรังสี
| Time | Usage |
|---|---|
| 1 min (nephrographic phase) | Assess renal parenchyma and contour and size |
| 5 min (early excretion) | Assess pelvicalyceal system & upper ureters |
| 10 min (pyelographic phase) | Assess well-distended pelvicalyceal system (5 min after compression) |
| 15 min (KUB radiograph) | Assess outline of ureters and bladder (immediate after release) |
| Full bladder | Assess bladder shape and outline |
| Post-void | Assess residual volume |
- Conventional IVP/IVU series ทำท่า supine ได้ภาพ AP · เพิ่ม oblique/lateral ± prone ในรายที่ก้ำกึ่งหรือสงสัย

## p18 CT urography — phases (Grainger & Allison's Diagnostic Radiology 7th ed 2021; Fried 2019)
| Time after contrast | Usage |
|---|---|
| Before (plain, non-contrast) | Presence of KUB stone, hydronephrosis, kidney size |
| 15–30/30–45 seconds (corticomedullary phase) | Hypervascular lesions (e.g., renal cell carcinoma) |
| 80–100/90–120 seconds (nephrographic phase) | Space-taking lesions in KUB |
| After 3 min (excretory phase) | Filling defect in urinary tract |

## p22 US KUB — ค่าปกติ (Hansen KL, Nielsen MB, Ewertsen C. Diagnostics 2016;6(1):2)
- Length (L): **10–12** (สไลด์พิมพ์ "mm" — ที่ถูกคือ **cm**) · cortical thickness (C): **7–10 mm** · parenchymal thickness (P): **15–20 mm**
- Renal cortex is normally **slightly hypoechoic compared to liver & spleen**
- Presence of **corticomedullary differentiation**

## p28 Plain radiograph — "Staghorn stone" (ภาพ · Imaging in Urology 1st ed 2018)

## p29 Plain radiograph of kidney stone (ACR Acute Onset Flank Pain–Suspicion of Stone Disease, revised 2023)
- Sensitivity **29%** for any stone of any size/location · **72%** for > 5 mm stone in proximal ureter
- Limitations: difficult to differentiate from calcification in other organs on single 2-D view (e.g., **phleboliths**) · not all stones radiopaque — **uric acid, cystine, indinavir, xanthine are radiolucent** (ตำราส่วนใหญ่: cystine = faintly radiopaque "ground glass" — บทเรียนบอกทั้งสองแบบ)

## p30 US of kidney stone (ภาพ)
- "Hyperechoic focus with **posterior acoustic shadow** in upper pole calyx"
- "**Twinkle artifact** in Doppler ultrasound"

## p31 CT of kidney stone (ภาพ)
- Corticomedullary phase: moderate left hydronephrosis
- Excretory phase: calculus in left middle ureter with "**soft tissue rim sign**"

## p32 CT of kidney stone (ACR 2023; Dell'Aversana F, Pezzullo M, Scaglione M. Urol Clin North Am 2025;52(1):51-59)
- Sensitivity **97%** · Specificity **95%** for any stone of any size/location
- Key advantages: exact number, size, location · differentiate from other calcifications (phleboliths) · complications (hydronephrosis) · surgical planning · guiding stone composition (**DECT**)

## p33–34 Selected Case 02
หญิงไทยอายุ 65 ปี เบาหวาน นอนโรงพยาบาล 7 วัน
- V/S: BT 39.2 °C · BP 105/65 · PR 120 · RR 24 · PE: right CVA tenderness
- Principal Dx: **E. coli acute pyelonephritis with septicemia** · Rx ceftriaxone × 7 วัน
- Outcome: **persistent fever, right back pain**

## p35 ACR — Acute pyelonephritis
Uncomplicated: USG / IVU / CT ± contrast / MRI — **usually not appropriate ทั้งหมด**
Complicated: USG **may be** · IVU usually not · **CT KUB with contrast usually appropriate** · MRI ± gad may be appropriate
- ✍️ ลายมือ (OCR): "= Abscess? / emph…" → อ่านได้ว่า **"Abscess? / emphysematous"** = สิ่งที่ต้องหาใน complicated APN

## p36 Emphysematous pyelonephritis (ภาพ · Imaging in Urology 2018)
- Plain film: **curvilinear gas collection outlining the lower pole of the left kidney**
- CT: large gas-fluid collections in the left renal parenchyma

## p37 EPN — US
- "Non-dependent **dirty shadowing** echoes associated within the renal parenchyma" · CT: large gas-fluid collections in right kidney

## p38 Renal abscess (Comprehensive Clinical Nephrology 7th ed 2024)
- US: **enlarged hyperechoic right kidney without mass/stone/HN**
- CT: **multiple non-enhancing hypodense lesions**, varying in size, in right kidney → compatible with right renal abscess

## p39 Renal abscess
- US: single **complex mixed anechoic–hypoechoic round lesion with posterior enhancement** at upper pole right kidney
- CT: large round hypodense lesion with **thin rim-enhancing wall** at upper pole right kidney

## p42 Ultrasonography of kidney failure — AKI vs CKD (Petrucci I, Clementi A, Sessa C, et al. J Nephrol 2018;31:863-879)
| Findings | AKI | CKD |
|---|---|---|
| Appearance & surface | Regular, smooth, well-defined | May be irregular ± acquired cysts involving cortex & medulla or ADPKD appearance |
| Kidney size (longitudinal, cm) | ↔ (10–12), ↑ | ↓ (progressive) |
| Shape & volume | Globular, swollen, volume ↔, ↑ | ↓ (progressive) · shrunken in ESKD |
| Echogenicity & texture | Varied depends on causes | **Hyperechoic due to fibrosis** |
| Pyramids/medulla | May be hypoechoic, dysmorphic | Not seen (due to homogenization) |
| Renal cortex | Varied depends on causes | **Thin (< 10 mm)** ± cysts ± cortical nephrocalcinosis |
| Renal sinus | ± compressed, dislocated | Sclerolipomatosis |
- ✍️ มีลายมือเหนือตาราง (OCR "udoomephotic" — อ่านไม่ออก)

## p44 Grading systems of hydronephrosis (ภาพตาราง/ภาพวาดระดับ — มองไม่เห็น) · Ultrasonography in post-renal AKI
(Feliciangeli V, Noce A, Montalto G, et al. Clin Kidney J 2024;17(10):sfae249 · Onen A. Front Pediatr 2020;8:458 · Timberlake MD, Herndon CDA. Nat Rev Urol 2013;10:649-56)
**Obstruction WITHOUT hydronephrosis**
- Early obstruction
- Severe volume depletion / oliguria / anuria
- Retroperitoneal fibrosis ∂
- TB KUB ∂
- Infiltrative / chronic kidney disease ∂
- Partial obstruction
∂ = ไม่ได้ทำให้เกิด AKI เอง แต่อาจมี AKI ซ้อนบนภาวะเหล่านี้
**!! There are conditions causing "nonobstructive hydronephrosis"** (สไลด์ไม่ได้ยกตัวอย่าง → บทเรียนเติม: VUR, pregnancy, full bladder, diuresis/DI, post-obstructive dilatation, extrarenal pelvis, megacalycosis/PUJ capacious system, parapelvic cysts mimic)
