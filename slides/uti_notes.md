## ช่วง 3 · Definition
- Presence of bacteria or other microorganisms in the urine or genitourinary tissues
- **≠ bacteriuria · ≠ pyuria**
- Definitive diagnosis and management usually require **microbiologic confirmation by urine culture**
- **Urine culture ก่อนเริ่มยาเสมอ** — urinary excretion of antimicrobials rapidly sterilizes urine
- ผู้หญิงมักมี contaminating organisms จาก vaginal/periurethral flora ใน voided specimen → ใช้ **quantitative criterion** แยก bacteriuria จาก contamination

## ช่วง 4 · Significant bacteriuria (Brenner & Rector 11th ed.)
- Standard quantitative criterion (voided specimen): **≥10⁵ CFU/mL of a potential uropathogen**
- ✍️ "**≥3 bacterias; suspected contamination**" (เพาะขึ้น ≥ 3 ชนิด = สงสัยปนเปื้อน)
- สไลด์ถัดไป 2 หน้า (อ้าง Brenner & Rector) เป็น **ภาพ/ตาราง อ่านไม่ได้** — น่าจะเป็นตารางเกณฑ์ CFU ตามชนิดสิ่งส่งตรวจ → เติมจาก IDSA (ระบุในบทเรียน)

## ช่วง 5 · Urinalysis
- "Accuracy — **Epithelial cells < 5/HPF = Good urine sample**"
- ✍️ เพี้ยน: "Hematuria … Non-fla…" · "↓ contaminate" → ตีความ: squamous epithelial cells มาก = ปนเปื้อน
- ตาราง/ภาพ UA บนสไลด์นี้ **อ่านไม่ได้**

**Dipstick**
- **Leukocyte esterase** — corresponds to pyuria · detects **WBC ≥ 10 cells/HPF** · **sensitivity 75–96% · specificity 94–98%**
- **Positive nitrite test** — reliable index of significant bacteriuria · E. coli, Proteus mirabilis, Klebsiella produce **nitrate reductase** that transforms nitrate of alimentary origin into nitrite · **Negative test does not exclude bacteriuria**
- ✍️ "= Support …" (อ่านไม่ออก)

**Microscopy**
- **Pyuria: WBC ≥ 5 cells/HPF in centrifuged sample (≥ 10 cells/HPF in uncentrifuged sample)**
- **Caution in neutropenic patients!!** ✍️ "↳ infection …" (ตีความ: ไม่มี WBC ก็ติดเชื้อได้)
- **WBC cast**

**Sterile pyuria = pyuria without bacteriuria**
- Incomplete antimicrobial treatment
- Other infection — TB, anaerobe, mycoplasma, chlamydia, virus, fungus, schistosomiasis
- Interstitial nephritis, cystitis, allergic
- Uroepithelial inflammation — urolithiasis, foreign bodies
- Contamination from vaginal secretion
- Urinary tract neoplasm
- ✍️ "AIN ~ 'Partial treat'"

**Urine culture** — สไลด์เป็นภาพ อ่านไม่ได้
**Urine Gram stain** — สไลด์เป็นภาพ อ่านไม่ได้

## ช่วง 6 · Anatomy and physiology review · Pathogenesis
- Kidneys filter blood, produce urine · Ureters transport · Bladder stores · Urethra expels
- **Defense mechanisms: urine flow, mucosal immunity, antimicrobial properties of the urinary tract**
- **Ascending infection**: bacteria from the urethra ascend to the bladder and kidneys
- **Hematogenous spread**: less common, usually with systemic infections
- รูป pathogenesis (Trends in Molecular Medicine 2016;22:946–957) **เป็นภาพ อ่านไม่ได้** → เติมกลไก UPEC (type 1/P fimbriae, IBC) จาก Flores-Mireles Nat Rev Microbiol 2015 / ตำรา

## ช่วง 7 · Management (3 กลุ่ม): 1. Acute simple cystitis 2. Acute uncomplicated pyelonephritis 3. Complicated UTIs

### 1. Acute simple cystitis (Females)
- DDx: **Vaginitis / Urethritis / Painful bladder syndrome / PID**
- **First-line: Nitrofurantoin for 5 days**
- Alternative: **Single-dose Fosfomycin, Single-dose Aminoglycoside** · **Beta-lactam: amox/clav, cefixime for 7 days**
- Use with caution, resistance concerns: **Ciprofloxacin, Levofloxacin for 3 days · TMP/SMX for 3 days**

### 2. Acute uncomplicated pyelonephritis (Females)
- **Without SIRS/septic shock** — First-line **Ceftriaxone** · Alternative **Gentamicin, Amikacin**
- **With septicemia/septic shock** — First-line **Meropenem / Imipenem-cilastatin** · Alternative **Gentamicin, Amikacin**
  ✍️ "E. coli … → 'Beta-lactamase'" → ตีความ: กลัว **ESBL-producing E. coli** จึงใช้ carbapenem ในรายช็อก
- **Indications for hospitalization**: persistently high fever or pain · marked debility · inability to maintain oral hydration or take oral medications · urinary tract obstruction is suspected · concern about patient adherence
- **Step-down; switch to oral** (depends on culture/sensitivity): Ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin, TMP/SMX or Cefixime, Amox/clav
- **Total duration: 7 days (oral cephalosporin 10–14 days)**

### UTIs: clinical response to antimicrobial therapy
- **Acute simple cystitis: within 48 hours**
- **Acute uncomplicated UTI (pyelo): 48–72 hours**
- **Imaging is generally reserved for**: severely ill · persistent clinical symptoms despite appropriate antimicrobial therapy · **suspected urinary tract obstruction** (e.g., renal function declined below baseline or precipitous decline in urine output) · recurrent symptoms; relapse within a few weeks of treatment

### 3. Complicated UTI
- **Males · Comorbidities — DM, immunocompromised · Pregnancy · Kidney transplant recipients · Recurrent uncomplicated UTIs**
- **No response to treatment within 72 hrs / suspected urologic obstruction → Imaging · consider longer duration of therapy**
- Empiric: **First-line Ceftriaxone** · Alternative **gentamicin, amikacin** · **Nosocomial/MDR risk: Pip/tazobactam, Ceftazidime, Cefepime**
- Definitive: step-down to oral based on susceptibility — Ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin, TMP/SMX, beta-lactam
- **Total duration 10–14 days, or longer for specific indications**

## ช่วง 8 · Recurrent UTIs
- **≥ 2 UTIs in 6 months or ≥ 3 UTIs in 12 months**
- **Reinfection** — recurrence with the same or different organisms, **> 2 weeks**
- **Relapse** — recurrence with **same uropathogen strain within 2 weeks**
- **Failure to eradicate infection: stones, scarred kidney, PKD, prostatitis**
- Risk factors: **Behavioral** — sexual intercourse, diaphragm-spermicide · **Urologic** — urinary incontinence, cystocele, post-voiding residual urine · **Biologic/genetic**
- Management: changes in behavior · liberal fluid intake · contraception modification · postcoital voiding · hygiene · **topical estrogen for postmenopausal women** · ultrasound or cystoscopy imaging may be indicated

## ช่วง 9 · Catheter-associated UTI (CA-UTI)
- Indwelling catheter + quantitative criteria: **Asymptomatic ≥ 10⁵ CFU/mL · Symptomatic ≥ 10² CFU/mL** (ตามข้อความที่ดึงได้ "≥102" = 10²)
  (IDSA 2009 CAUTI ใช้ ≥ 10³ CFU/mL สำหรับรายมีอาการ — แสดงทั้งสองค่าในบทเรียน)
- ✍️ "~ Biofilm"
- Prevention strategies: **off catheter → clean intermittent catheterization (CIC)** · insertion techniques and drainage consideration · **catheter exchange timing: every 4 weeks** · catheter materials and coatings · antibiotic prophylaxis (?) , probiotics (Research and Reports in Urology 2022:14 109–133)

## ช่วง 10 · ภาพประกอบ (ภาพล้วน)
- **Stone** — CT KUB (litfl.com) ✍️ "Radio = Ca" · "uric stone" → ตีความ: นิ่วแคลเซียมทึบรังสี · นิ่วกรดยูริกโปร่งรังสี (เห็นใน CT)
- **Hydronephrosis** (ภาพ)
- **Renal and perinephric abscess**: **US — hypoechoic mass indicative of pyelonephritis or early abscess** · **Contrast-enhanced CT — triangular hypodense streaks spreading from the pelvis to the renal cortex** (= pyelonephritis) · **Contrast CT — large, hypodense region** (= abscess) · ✍️ "E. coli"
- **Infected renal cysts** — ภาพ US (McGahan 2008; Weber 2006): Complicated ADPKD → bleeding / infection / malignant tumor · echogenic cyst · aspiration reveals blood vs pus (patient pyrexial) · solid renal mass = papillary RCC

## ช่วง 11 · UTI ใน ADPKD (KDIGO ADPKD 2025)
- ✍️ "… Leukocytosis, fever, …"
- Cystitis/pyelonephritis: dysuria, frequency
- **Kidney cyst infection: fever, flank pain** · DDx **cyst hemorrhage, kidney stone**
- Pathogens: **E. coli (75%)**, K. pneumoniae, Enterococcus species
- Urine culture before antibiotics
- **Cyst aspiration whenever possible** — confirm infection + therapeutic drainage
- Imaging usually necessary: **Ultrasonography is not useful** · **CT/MRI** localize + anatomy but **not specific** · **WBC-labeled nuclear scans** — FP/FN possible · **FDG-PET most effective but limited access** · **Cyst aspiration** when clinical + imaging suggestive and blood/urine cultures negative (Clinical and Translational Imaging 2018;6:61–67)
- **KDIGO algorithm (Figure 17)**: Suspected cyst infection = **fever > 38 °C** + **acute abdominal pain** + **CRP ≥ 50 mg/L** (ข้อความดึงได้เป็น "250 mg/l" — ตีความว่า ≥ 50) **or WBC > 11 × 10⁹/L**
  → other source of infection/inflammation? (imaging US/CT/MRI) → ถ้ามี = cyst infection unlikely
  → ถ้าไม่มี → **diagnostic features positive (≥ 2 items in ≥ 2 categories)** → likely kidney cyst infection → confirmation required? yes → **18-FDG PET-CT** · no → no additional imaging
  Diagnostic features: Clinical (1 acute pain/tenderness kidney area · 2 UTI symptoms · 3 recent instrumentation · 4 immunocompromised incl. dialysis) · Microbiology (5 positive urine/blood culture · 6 positive cyst fluid culture) · Imaging (7 new complex cyst · 8 intracystic gas · 9 pericystic inflammation · 10 fluid-fluid level MRI · 11 thickened wall · 12 wall enhancement · 13 DWI ↑ density · 14 Ga-67 SPECT · 15 In-111 WBC scan) · Treatment (16 clinical response to antibiotics)
- **Treatment: lipid-soluble (lipophilic) antibiotics** — **fluoroquinolones (levofloxacin > ciprofloxacin), TMP/SMX** · **duration 4–6 weeks** · fever persists → **percutaneous or surgical drainage** · nephrectomy occasionally

## ช่วง 12 · Emphysematous cystitis and pyelonephritis
- Infections of lower or upper urinary tract **associated with gas formation** · ✍️ "gas forming"
- ภาพ (a)–(e) อ่านไม่ได้
- **Risk: Diabetes mellitus** · **Pathogens: E. coli or K. pneumoniae (65–100%)**
- Clinical: **severe acute pyelonephritis** · **pneumaturia and palpable crepitus < 10%** · **AKI 60%** · **septic shock 19%**
- "Gas forming & necrotizing infection" · Risk factors: **Diabetes · urinary tract obstruction (renal/ureteric stones, papillary necrosis)** · Microbiology E. coli, Klebsiella
- **CT scan KUB is the diagnostic modality of choice**
- **Prognostic classification** (#NephroVisuals @DrPSVali — Huang & Tseng): Class I gas in collecting system · Class II gas in renal parenchyma · Class IIIA extension into perinephric space (between capsule & fascia) · Class IIIB extension into pararenal space (beyond renal fascia) · Class IV gas in both kidneys or solitary functioning kidney (ข้อความดึงได้เป็น "Class V" — ตามต้นฉบับ Huang & Tseng 2000 คือ Class IV)

## ช่วง 13 · Xanthogranulomatous pyelonephritis (XPN)
- Unusual variant of **chronic pyelonephritis** · almost always **unilateral** · virtually **complete destruction of the kidney** · **DDx renal carcinoma** (clinical + radiographic)
- Histology: interstitial infiltrate of neutrophils, mononuclear cells, and most characteristically **lipid-laden macrophages** · ✍️ "calcium stone", "chronic infect."
- Presentation: **middle-aged females with history of recurrent UTI** · **obstruction due to infected kidney stones** · flank pain, fever, malaise, anorexia, weight loss · **unilateral kidney mass** · Labs: **anemia, ↑ESR** · UA pyuria, bacteriuria
- Pathogens: E. coli, **Proteus mirabilis**, Pseudomonas, Enterococcus faecalis, Klebsiella · **Sterile urine cultures 25–40%**
- CT: enlarged (12.2 × 9.1 cm) left kidney, dilated pelvis/calyces, multiloculated — **"bear's paw"** = typical

## ช่วง 14 · Genitourinary TB
- Usually involves urinary tract or genital organs due to **hematogenous spread of chronic latent pulmonary TB**
- **Diagnosis often delayed** — symptoms masked by another disease, usually UTI
- Pathogenesis of urinary TB (RadioGraphics 2021;41:1123–1143) — ภาพ
- Imaging: Adrenal — enlargement/peripheral enhancement in Addison disease + TB · Kidney — peripheral lobar calcifications · **blunting of calyces (caliectasis) and long ureteral strictures** · **small nonfunctioning right kidney containing calcific debris** · Prostate — large abscesses in HIV + TB · Fallopian tubes — diverticular outpouching

## ช่วง 15 · Funguria
- Common in hospitalized patients, **generally benign** · Pathogen mostly **Candida albicans** and other Candida spp.
- **Infection vs colonization** · dysuria, frequency, suprapubic discomfort · **chronic indwelling bladder catheter**
- **Asymptomatic candiduria — usually no treatment**
- **Treat if high risk of dissemination: neutropenia, very low birthweight infants (< 1500 g), urinary tract manipulation**
- **Symptomatic candiduria: Fluconazole** · **Amphotericin B if any risk for fluconazole resistance**

## ช่วง 16 · Asymptomatic bacteriuria
- Isolation of bacteria **≥ 10⁵ CFU/mL in a voided clean-catch specimen without symptoms** of UTI
- Most have **no adverse consequences and derive no benefit** from antibiotic therapy
- **NOT routinely screening or treating ASB**
- Antimicrobial treatment only beneficial in: **Pregnancy · Patients undergoing urologic intervention · Renal transplant recipients** (สไลด์)
  (IDSA 2019 ASB: แนะนำ **ไม่** คัดกรอง/รักษา ASB ในผู้รับไตที่ผ่าตัดเกิน 1 เดือน — แสดงทั้งสองแนวในบทเรียน)
