## PE (ดู-คลำ-เคาะ-ฟัง)
- Inspection: chest wall abnormalities (pectus — สัมพันธ์ MVP/Marfan), pulsations · Percussion: cardiac dullness ขนาด/รูปหัวใจ
- Palpation: apical impulse ปกติ left 5th ICS MCL Ø <3 cm · >3 cm = diffuse · apical impulse ≠ PMI · LV/RV heave = hypertrophy/dilatation · **thrill = murmur ≥ grade 4/6**
- Auscultation 4 areas (AVA, PVA, TVA, MVA) · S1 = M1+T1 (AV valves close, "lub") · S2 = A2+P2 ("dub")
- บรรยาย murmur: systole/diastole · configuration & frequency · intensity · area & radiation — ตัวอย่าง "pansystolic murmur grade II at MVA, radiation to axilla"
- Systolic: AS PS (ejection) · MR TR (pansystolic) · Diastolic: AR PR (blowing) · MS TS (rumbling)

## MS
- Etiology: **rheumatic (ส่วนใหญ่)** · congenital · severe MAC · SLE/RA · LA myxoma · IE large vegetation
- Pathophys: ↑LA pressure → LA enlargement → AF · pulmonary venous HT → PA HT → RV enlargement/failure · ↓CO
- Sx: dyspnea · hemoptysis · thrombi/emboli · HF · dysphagia (LA กดหลอดอาหาร) · hoarseness (**Ortner's syndrome**)
- PE: malar flush (mitral facies) · **loud S1 · opening snap · low-pitched diastolic rumbling murmur**
- Associated: PH → loud P2, RV heave · TR (PSM LLSB ดังขึ้นตอนหายใจเข้า = **Carvallo's sign**) · PR (DBM LUSB = **Graham Steell**) · AS AR TS (RHD)
- ECG: LAE (P mitrale), RVH, RAD, AF · CXR: LA/LAA enlargement (widening carina angle, double contour, enlarged LAA, esophagus compression), PA/RV/RA enlargement, Kerley B
- TTE: severity + suitability for **PMBV/PMBC**
- Rx: 2nd RF prophylaxis · CHF: Na restriction diuretics · BB/CCB/digoxin rate AF · **warfarin ใน AF หรือ Hx thromboembolism**
- ACC/AHA (2020): severe MS **MVA ≤1.5 cm² (T½ ≥150 ms)** · very severe ≤1.0 (T½ ≥220) · progressive >1.5
  - symptomatic (D) + favorable morphology, no LA clot, no/mild MR → **PMBC (I)** · not favorable + high surgical risk NYHA III–IV → PMBC (IIb) else **MVR (I)**
  - asymptomatic severe (C) + favorable → PMBC (IIa) · new AF → PMBC (IIb) · progressive + symptomatic + PCWP >25 with exercise → PMBC (IIb)
- Intervention ทั่วไป: severe stenosis/regurgitation + symptomatic หรือ asymptomatic + poor prognostic factor

## MR
- Acute: papillary muscle rupture post-MI · blunt trauma · IE · ruptured chordae · Chronic: RHD · MVP · congenital cleft · HOCM · DCM
- Primary (valve) vs secondary (LV/atrial)
- Sx: mild-mod asymptomatic · severe: fatigue exertional dyspnea orthopnea · acute: pulmonary edema
- PE chronic: apex displaced laterally · soft/absent S1 · holosystolic murmur MVA (radiate axilla)
- ECG LA/LV enlargement, AF · CXR LA LV enlarged · Echo: etiology, LV function/dimension, repairability
- Severe MR: **VC ≥0.7 cm · RVol ≥60 mL · RF ≥50% · ERO ≥0.4 cm²** · LV dilation
- Primary MR: symptomatic (D) → MV surgery (I) · asymptomatic (C2) **LVEF ≤60% or LVESD ≥40** → surgery (I) · C1 (LVEF >60, LVESD <40) + repair success >95% + mortality <1% → repair (IIa) · C1 + new AF or PASP >50 → surgery (IIa) · progressive B → monitoring
- Secondary MR: CAD Rx, HF Rx (GDMT), CRT; persistent NYHA III–IV → MV surgery (IIb) / TEER (ใหม่)
- MVP: mid/late systolic click + late systolic murmur · myxomatous, collagen · thoracic skeletal abnormalities, Marfan · F>M · asymptomatic/atypical chest pain/palpitation/CHF · symptomatic severe MR → MV repair

## AS
- Etiology: degenerative calcification · congenital bicuspid/unicuspid · rheumatic
- Symptoms (ภาพ — angina, syncope, HF) · PE: ESM 2nd RICS radiate carotids · LV heave
- ECG LVH strain, LAD · CXR little enlargement, dilated proximal ascending aorta, AV calcification
- Severe AS: avoid competitive sports & dehydration · (แผนภูมิ AS เป็นภาพ — ไม่มีข้อความ)

## AR
- Valve: rheumatic, bicuspid, cusp prolapse, IE, trauma, syphilis/AS(ankylosing spondylitis) · Root: Marfan, dissection, HT, aortitis
- Sx: exertional dyspnea, angina, HF · LV heave, apex displaced laterally+inferiorly
- **DBM best at 3rd left ICS → valve disease · 2nd right ICS → root disease**
- Chronic severe: wide pulse pressure, water-hammer, de Musset, Corrigan, Quincke, Müller, Traube, Duroziez
- Severe AR: **VC >0.6 · holodiastolic aortic flow reversal · RVol ≥60 · RF ≥50% · ERO ≥0.3** · LV dilation
- AVR: symptomatic (I) · asymptomatic LVEF <50% (C2, I) · other cardiac surgery (I) · LVEF ≥50% + LVESD >50 (IIa) · LVEDD >65 + low risk (IIb) · progressive B + other cardiac surgery (IIa)

## TR/TS/PR/PS
- TR primary: RHD, IE, carcinoid, trauma, papillary injury · secondary: PH · TS: RHD, congenital
- PR primary: RHD, congenital · secondary: PH, idiopathic dilatation, Marfan · PS: congenital, carcinoid

## ARF
- ภาพ: antibody cross-reactivity (molecular mimicry)
- หลัง GAS pharyngitis/scarlet fever ที่รักษาไม่พอ · pharyngitis 2–4 wk ก่อน · อายุ 5–15 ปี
- Jones: 2 major หรือ 1 major + 2 minor + evidence of antecedent GAS
- Major: **erythema marginatum · polyarthritis · Sydenham chorea (St. Vitus' dance) · carditis · subcutaneous nodule** — จำ "ขอบ-ข้อ-คอ-คำ-คว"? (สไลด์: ขอบ-ขอ-คอ-คำ-คว)
- Minor: fever · arthralgia · prolonged PR · ↑ESR/CRP
- GAS evidence: throat culture/rapid antigen + · ASO / anti-DNase B สูงหรือขึ้น
- Carditis 40–60% · pancarditis · valvulitis: apical PSM (MR), apical short DRM (**Carey Coombs**), basal DBM (AR) · myocarditis: cardiomegaly CHF · pericarditis: rub, effusion
- WHO 2002–2003: primary/recurrent without RHD → Jones · recurrent with established RHD → 2 minor + GAS evidence · isolated chorea or indolent carditis → ไม่ต้องมี major อื่นหรือ GAS evidence
- Revised Jones ACC/AHA 2015 (ตารางภาพ) · Doppler findings rheumatic valvulitis (ภาพ)
- Rx: symptomatic relief · eradicate GAS · prophylaxis
- GAS pharyngitis: BPG 1.2 MU (สไลด์เขียน 1–2 MU) IM once; 600,000 U ถ้า <27 kg · Pen V/amoxicillin เด็ก 250 bid, ผู้ใหญ่ 500 bid ×10 d · แพ้ penicillin: cephalosporin/erythromycin 10 d
- Secondary: BPG 1–2 MU (1.2 MU) IM q3–4 wk · Pen V 250 bid · erythromycin 250 bid
- Duration: carditis + residual valve disease → ≥10 y since last episode and until age ≥40, sometimes lifelong · carditis no residual → 10 y or until 21 (longer) · no carditis → 5 y or until 21 (longer)
- Symptomatic: arthritis salicylate 75 mg/kg/d 4–6 wk · carditis no cardiomegaly salicylate 90–100 mg/kg/d 4–8 wk · carditis + cardiomegaly/CHF prednisolone 2 mg/kg/d ×2 wk then salicylate 90–100 ×6 wk
- Home message: VHD Rx symptomatic (CHF) · intervention = severe + symptom/bad prognostic factor · ARF: Jones, GAS eradication, 2nd prophylaxis, anti-inflammation
