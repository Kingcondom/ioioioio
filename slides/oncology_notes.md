- Topics: incidence · core concept of treatment · normal vs cancer cell · chemotherapy · targeted therapy · common cancer (breast)

- Aims: early → cure, curative surgery, neoadjuvant (downsize), adjuvant (prolong DFS/OS) · advanced → prolong survival with QoL, palliative chemo/RT, symptom control, favorable toxicity
- Colon: surgery stage I–II 5-y 85% · stage III 30–50% · adjuvant 5FU 10–20% · + oxaliplatin additional 5% (6-y)
- Patient factors: PS, comorbidity, well-being, chronological vs functional age, SES, attitude, psychological, support · Karnofsky table 100–0 (≥80 normal activity; 50–70 unable to work, home; ≤40 unable to self-care)
- Cancer factors: stage · chemo-responsive type · high grade, mitosis, Ki-67, poorly differentiated · radiosensitive seminoma lymphoma SCC · predictive HER2, KRAS, EGFR
- Normal cell: most in G0, need growth signal · ligand receptor downstream checkpoint · EGF/EGFR HER family · checkpoints G1 (restriction), G2, metaphase · DNA damage → stall/repair or apoptosis
- Hallmarks: self-sufficiency growth signal, insensitivity antigrowth, evade apoptosis, limitless replication, angiogenesis, invasion/metastasis · seed & soil · new hallmarks 2011 (Cell)
- Carcinogenesis pathways: secrete signal, ↑receptor, receptor mutation, autonomous downstream · angiogenesis VEGF · apoptosis · metastasis · multifactorial germline/sporadic, environment smoking UV · multistep · precancerous dysplasia = IEN → CIS · cervix CIN
- Antineoplastic: cytotoxic — non cell-cycle (steroid), non-phase specific (platinum, alkylating ifosfamide cyclophosphamide), phase-specific S (5FU antimetabolite, topoisomerase inhibitor e.g. anthracycline), G2 bleomycin, M taxane vinca · targeted
- Cytotoxic active only in dividing; turnover days–weeks vs exposure hours · cancer factors rapid growth, poorly diff, high growth fraction, Ki-67 (germ cell, SCLC) · chemo factors exposure time (5FU infusion > push), generation (3rd gen NSCLC), combination (platinum doublet)
- Targeted: mab upstream, nib downstream TKI · TK receptor (EGFR VEGFR PDGFR) vs non-receptor (JAK2, c-ABL) · HER: cetuximab panitumumab trastuzumab pertuzumab; erlotinib gefitinib lapatinib imatinib
- Angiogenesis: tumor 1–2 mm avascular dormant → angiogenic switch VEGF overexpression · tumor vessels leaky · bevacizumab, VEGF-trap, TKIs sorafenib pazopanib
- IO: positive/negative signal · CTLA4 early T-cell activation · PD1 effector phase · approvals: melanoma, NSCLC, SCLC, RCC, UC, HNSCC, cHL, PMBCL, HCC, gastric, cervical, TNBC, Merkel, cSCC, MSI-H CRC, dMMR solid · irAEs kinetics (Nat Rev Clin Oncol 2019)
- Breast: yearly mammogram 40–50 · HBOC AD (<45, bilateral, male, + ovarian) BRCA1/2 → MMG/MRI from 30 · IDC/ILC · ER/PR+, HER2+ (3+ or FISH), TNBC · worst prognosis LN status · older/postmenopausal favorable
- Early: MRM or BCS+RT · axilla ALND or SLNB (cN0) · adj RT LN>4, T>5 cm, close margin · adj endocrine 5 yr (premeno TAM, postmeno AI ± TAM) any ER/PR+ · adj chemo anthracycline LN+ or >1–2 cm · trastuzumab 1 yr HER2+
- Locally advanced/inflammatory: chemo (anthracycline) → MRM → RT
- Metastatic HR+: bone/soft tissue/asymptomatic minimal visceral → endocrine (premeno TAM, postmeno AI/TAM) · massive symptomatic visceral → chemo (naive anthracycline; prior A taxane ± trastuzumab) · HR− → chemo
