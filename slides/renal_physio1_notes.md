## 1. Renal function (7 ข้อ)
1. ขับของเสีย: creatinine (creatine), urea (amino acid), uric acid (purine) — ไตเสีย → ↑Scr BUN (azotemia) → uremic sx: N/V, fatigue, muscle cramp · ↑uric acid → gout (สไลด์พิมพ์ "Goat")
2. ขับสารแปลกปลอม/ยา — ไตเสีย → ↓ renal drug clearance → drug intoxication
3. Electrolyte: ขับ Na K Mg P ส่วนเกินจากอาหาร · **conserve Ca** — ไตเสีย: Na retention → fluid → HT/edema · hyperK → arrhythmia · hyperPi · ↓1α-hydroxylase → ↓calcitriol → ↓gut Ca absorption + ↓renal Ca reabsorption → hypoCa
4. Body fluid volume/osmolality — ไตเสีย ขับน้ำไม่ได้ → HT, edema
5. Endocrine: 5.1 renin (RAAS → BP) · 5.2 EPO (interstitial fibroblasts ใน peritubular capillary bed ของ cortex และ outer medulla; กระตุ้นโดย low interstitial PaO2 จาก hypoxemia/hypoperfusion) → marrow RBC — ไตเสีย → anemia (fatigue, pale) · 5.3 1α-hydroxylase (PT cell) 25(OH)D3 → 1,25(OH)2D3 — ไตเสีย → hypoCa → ↑PTH → bone resorption → renal osteodystrophy · 5.4 PGI2/PGE2 + 5.5 kinin → local vasodilation
6. Acid–base: H+ secretion, HCO3- reabsorption & production — ไตเสีย → metabolic acidosis
7. Gluconeogenesis — glutamine metabolism ใน PT (เชื่อมกับ NH4+/HCO3- production)
Overview: renal artery → nephron (plasma → urine) → pelvis → ureter → bladder → urethra (ภาพ)

## 2. RBF
- 20% CO ขณะพัก · RBF ~1–1.2 L/min (ภายหลังเขียน 1.2–1.3 L/min)
- RBF ขึ้นกับ total renal vascular resistance โดยเฉพาะ arteriole: ↓diameter → ↑R → ↓RBF
- (1) Sympathetic: fiber เลี้ยงตั้งแต่ interlobar a. ถึง efferent; **afferent มี sympathetic + α1 มากกว่า (\*\*\*)**; ↓BV/BP, exercise, fear, fight → ↑SNS → afferent constriction (major site) → ↓RBF
- (2) Vasoactive substances (ตาราง):
  - NE, E (α1-AR), ATP (P2X), adenosine (A1R) → **afferent** constriction (major) ↓RBF
  - Endothelin, TXA2, ADH, high-dose dopamine → aff + eff constriction ↓
  - **Angiotensin II → efferent constriction (efferent more sensitive than afferent \*\*\*)** ↓RBF
  - NO, low-dose dopamine → aff + eff dilation ↑
  - PGI2, PGE2, ANP, bradykinin, kinin → **afferent** dilation (major) ↑
- (3) RAAS: angiotensinogen (liver) —renin (kidney)→ AI —ACE (lung)→ AII → vasoconstriction ↓RBF
  - Renin secretion triggers ที่ JG cell: 1) ↑SNS (β1-AR) — exercise/fear/fight, hypovolemia/hypotension → volume receptor/baroreceptor · 2) ↓renal perfusion pressure → ↓JG cell stretch (renal baroreceptor) — hypovolemia, RAS · 3) ↓NaCl delivery to macula densa — ↓GFR (glomerular disease, hypovolemia) or ↑NaCl reabsorption at PT/loop (high plasma glucose or amino acid) · **ANP inhibits (−)**

## 2b. Clinical assessment of RBF — Clearance
- Clearance = volume of plasma cleared of a substance per unit time · C = UA×V/PA (mL/min)
- RPF marker: freely filtered + rapidly & completely secreted → PAH: 20% filtered, 70% secreted, 10% returns to blood → C_PAH = 90% RPF = **ERPF ~630 mL/min** · extraction ratio 0.9 · ARPF = ERPF/0.9 ~700 mL/min
- Hct definition (40% = RBC 40 mL/100 mL) · RBF = ARPF/(1−Hct) ~1.2–1.3 L/min

## 3. Renal nerve supply
- celiac plexus → sympathetic → NE (& dopamine — counterbalance)
- NE: VSM α1 → afferent constriction ↓RBF ↓GFR · ECs & GMC α1 → PGE2/PGI2 release → vasodilation (counterbalance) · JG β1 → renin → AII → efferent constriction · GMC α1 → contraction ↓GC area ↓GFR · tubular α1 (PT, TAL, DT, CD) → ↑Na reabsorption
- intensity: normal → minimal; mild–moderate → mild (autoregulation); strong acute → RBF ↓ to 10–30% of normal

## 4. Urine formation = filtration + reabsorption + secretion
### Glomerular filtration — factors: membrane permeability, solute property, pressure gradient
- Endothelium fenestration 70 nm, negative glycoprotein → size barrier → blocks RBC WBC platelet
- GBM: porous negatively charged matrix (collagen IV) → size + **primary charge-selective** barrier → blocks large protein
- Slit diaphragm 4–14 nm, nephrin, NEPH-1, podocin (negative) → size + charge → blocks small protein (albumin)
- DM foot process widening → albuminuria · GC membrane impairment → hematuria + proteinuria
- Size: <3.6 nm/<5.5 kDa freely · 3.6–8.4 nm/5.5–70 kDa progressive ↓ · >8.4 nm/>70 kDa not filtered
- Filterability table (Boron & Boulpaep 2016): Na 0.20 nm 0.023 kDa 100 · urea 0.32/0.060 100 · H2O 0.30/0.018 100 · Cl 0.36 100 · glucose 0.66 nm 0.18 kDa 100 · creatinine 0.12 kDa 100 · **myoglobin 3.76 nm 17 kDa 75** · **hemoglobin 6.5 nm 68 kDa 3** · **albumin 7.1 nm 69 kDa 0.5**
- Charge: only macromolecule filterability affected; anionic albumin restricted; small ions freely; loss of negative glycoprotein (DM, nephrotic syndrome: immune damage or nephrin/podocin mutation) → albuminuria
- Plasma vs filtrate table (บางส่วน garbled): small solutes filtrate = plasma (glucose 100 mg/dL, amino acid 4.2, urea 25, uric acid 4, creatinine 1 mg/dL, osmolarity 300); albumin 4 g/dL → filtrate <0.02; RBC/WBC 0
- Filtrate contains water, glucose, AA, ions, urea, some hormones, vit B C, ketones, very small protein
### Pressure gradient
- NFP = PGC − (PBC + πGC) = 60 − (18 + 32) = 10 mmHg (πBC ≈ 0) · P depends on blood volume; π on plasma protein (albumin)
- GFR = Kf × NFP = 12.5 × 10 = **125 mL/min = 180 L/day**
- Physical variation: age >30 ↓, >80 ↓50% · ↑lean mass ↑ · men > women 10% · African-American > · high protein/CHO diet ↑ (autoregulation) · pregnancy ↑
- Kf: GMC contraction (SNS, NE, E, AII; also adenosine/ATP shown) ↓area ↓Kf ↓GFR · dopamine, ANP, NO, PGE2 → relax ↑ · GMC hypertrophy (DM) ↓ · hypoxia/toxic agents → endothelial contraction + TJ damage → ↑permeability ↑Kf ↑GFR (ตามสไลด์) · GBM thickening (DM, HT) ↓
- PGC: afferent constriction → ↓PGC ↓RBF ↓GFR · **efferent constriction (AII) → ↑PGC ↑GFR, ↓RBF — AII maintains GFR in hypoperfusion** · afferent dilation (PGI2, PGE2, ANP) ↑PGC ↑GFR ↑RBF — **PG maintains RBF in hemorrhage/shock; NSAID (ibuprofen, aspirin) inhibit PG → afferent constriction → AKI in hemorrhagic shock** · both dilated (NO, low-dose dopamine) ↑RBF ↔ PGC ↔ GFR (fig shows ↑↑ RBF) · efferent dilation → ↓PGC ↓GFR ↑RBF — **ARB/ACEi used to treat hyperfiltration in early diabetic nephropathy**
- PBC: kidney stone → pelvic/ureteral obstruction; prostate enlargement → urethral obstruction → backflow → ↑PBC ↓GFR
- πGC: ↑albumin (high protein diet, volume contraction) ↑πGC ↓GFR · ↓albumin (cirrhosis, malnutrition, nephrotic) ↓πGC ↑GFR
### Clinical assessment of GFR
- Inulin = gold standard · CCr clinically · creatinine = muscle creatine catabolism, 1–2% of pool/day, constant · 24-h CCr = UCr×V/PCr · normal sCr 0.6–1.2 mg/dL
- eGFR to stage CKD: (1976) Cockcroft-Gault, (1999) MDRD, (2012) CKD-EPI — **สมการเป็นภาพ อ่านไม่ได้** ref Comprehensive Clinical Nephrology 5th ed 2015
### Autoregulation
- MAP 80–170 → RBF & GFR almost constant · intrinsic, independent of nerve/hormone
- Myogenic (3–10 s): arcuate, interlobular, afferent · ↑pressure → stretch-gated cation channel → Na influx → depolarization → VGCC → Ca influx → constriction · stabilizes with posture · prevents pressure injury (HT, DM, ESRD)
- TGF: JGA, maintains GFR; ↑NaCl delivery to distal tubule → (figure) afferent constriction
- Note: absent when MAP < 75 · not perfect · altered by hormones/SNS responding to ECF
- High protein & CHO diet ↑GFR · **hyperglycemia in DM activates TGF → hyperfiltration → diabetic nephropathy** (ภาพกลไกอ่านไม่ได้)
### Filtration fraction
- FF = GFR/RPF ~0.16–0.20
- table: afferent constriction (NE,E) RPF↓ PGC↓ GFR↓ FF↔ · efferent constriction (AII) RPF↓ PGC↑ GFR↑ FF↑ · aff+eff dilation (low-dose dopamine) RPF↑ PGC↔ GFR↔ FF↓ · ↓plasma protein RPF↔ πGC↓ GFR↑ FF↑ · ureteral stone RPF↔ PBC↑ GFR↓ FF↓
- FF and PT reabsorption: ↑FF → ↓Pc, ↑πc in peritubular capillary → ↑net reabsorption pressure → ↑PT reabsorption
- GTB: ↓BV → ↑SNS (aff+eff constriction) + ↑RAAS/AII (efferent) → ↓RPF, ↔GFR → ↑FF → ↓Pc ↑πc → ↑PT reabsorption

## 4.5 Tubular transport
- Structure: PT · loop (thin desc, thin asc, TAL) · DT (early, late) · CD (CCD, OMCD, IMCD); P cell 2/3, I cell 1/3
- Reabsorption: nutrient, electrolyte, certain substances · Secretion: metabolic waste, excess electrolyte, unwanted substance, drug · routes: paracellular, transcellular
- Medullary interstitial osmolality 300 → 600 → 900 → 1200 mOsm/L (papilla)
### PT (60–70% of filtrate; highly H2O & solute permeable; isosmotic)
- [Na]TF 140, [Na]i 10 · Na/K-ATPase basolateral · NHE (AII ↑) · Na-glucose (100%), Na-AA (100%), Na-Pi (80%), organic (SO4, lactate, urate) · AQP1 AQP3 · HCO3- 80%, urea 50% · H2O, Na, Cl, K, Ca 65% · Mg 30%
- **Fanconi syndrome** (slide: defect in transporters esp. Na/K-ATPase) → glucosuria, aminoaciduria, ↑HCO3- & phosphate excretion
### Glucose
- 99–100% at PT; SGLT2 + GLUT2 ("SGLT reach saturation")
- Glucosuria: plasma glucose > renal plasma threshold 180 mg/dL (DM) · filtered load (Pglu×GFR) > Tm 375 mg/min — pregnancy (slide: ↑FBS, ↑GFR, ↓Tm) · PT defect: Fanconi, familial renal glucosuria (SGLT2 defect)
### AA, peptide, protein (PT only)
- AA & di–tetrapeptide 100%: Na/AA coTx; PepT1 (H+/oligopeptide) · oligopeptide 5–25 AA (angiotensin, glutathione) → brush border hydrolysis → Na/AA + PepT2 · protein/peptide hormone (albumin, insulin) → receptor-mediated endocytosis → endosome → lysosome → AA · **easily saturated → ↑filtered protein → proteinuria**
### Urinary protein
- normal 30–150 mg/day or <8 mg/dL · 45% filtered plasma protein exceeding PT capacity (albumin 85% → <20 mg/day or <1 mg/dL; microglobulin, apoprotein, enzyme, peptide hormone 15%) · dipstick sensitivity 15–30 mg/dL albumin (positive in nephrotic, Fanconi) · 55% tubular synthesis/secretion (TAL & early DT): Tamm-Horsfall/uromodulin 90% (30–50 mg/day), IgA, α1 & β2 microglobulin, urokinase · transient: strenuous exercise, orthostatic, dehydration, UTI, febrile illness
### HCO3- reabsorption — NHE, CA, NBC1; acetazolamide (CA inhibitor) side effects: ↓Na K reabsorption → hypoNa hypoK · ↑flow & tubular Na → ↑K secretion at P cell → hypoK · ↓H+ secretion & HCO3- reabsorption → metabolic acidosis · **Type II RTA = defect in NBC**
### Pi: 80% PT NaPi-IIa, IIc → PTH, FGF-23 → internalization/destruction of NaPi-IIa → ↓Pi reabsorption · 10% DT
### Loop of Henle
- Thin desc: H2O 15% (water permeable, solute impermeable) → hyperosmotic (300 → 1200)
- Thin asc: water impermeable; passive NaCl reabsorption by gradient
- TAL: water impermeable; NKCC2; ROMK K back leak → **+8 mV lumen** → paracellular K Ca Mg (claudin-16, 19); CaSR basolateral (↑[Ca]) ; ADH ↑NKCC2 · Na Cl K Ca 25%, HCO3- 10%, **Mg 60%** · hyposmotic (diluting)
- Loop diuretics: furosemide, ethacrynic acid, bumetanide · side effects: hypoNa hypoK; ↑flow/Na → ↑K secretion at P cell → hypoK; ↓K back leak → ↓Ca Mg reabsorption → hypoCa, hypoMg
- **Bartter** = AR defect in NKCC2 (slide) · hypomagnesemia–hypercalciuria = AR mutation CLDN16/19
### Early DT (water impermeable; hyposmotic)
- NCC; Ca via apical channel + calbindin + NCX/PMCA basolateral (PTH, 1,25D ↑); Mg channel · NaCl ~5%, Ca 8%, Mg 5% · AII
- Thiazide: HCTZ, bendroflumethiazide · side effects: hypoNa hypoK; ↑flow → ↑K secretion; **low [Na]i → ↑NCX → ↓[Ca]i → ↑Ca reabsorption → hyperCa** · downregulate Mg channel → hypoMg
- **Gitelman** = AR defect in NCC
### Late DT & CCD (isosmotic? slide labels; water permeable only with ADH)
- P cell: AQP2 (ADH); high tubular Ca → CaSR inhibits AQP2 translocation → diuresis · Na reabsorption ENaC: aldosterone (+), ANP (−) · K secretion ROMK/BK: aldosterone (+), ↑flow & ↑tubular Na (+)
- I cell type A (acidosis): H+-ATPase (aldosterone +), H+/K+-ATPase (K reabsorption) · type B (alkalosis): Cl-/HCO3- (pendrin), K channel secretion
- K-sparing: ENaC blockers triamterene, amiloride; aldosterone antagonists spironolactone, eplerenone · side effects: hypoNa, **hyperK**, metabolic acidosis
- Liddle = AD gain of function ENaC · distal RTA (type I) = defect Cl-/HCO3- or H+-ATPase in type A I cell · NDI = impaired tubular response to ADH
### MCD
- H2O impermeable without ADH → hyposmotic urine; ADH → AQP2 → hyperosmotic; high Ca inhibits AQP2 · Na reabsorption (ANP inhibits) · K & H secretion · urea reabsorption UT-A1 (ADH) · NaCl 4% · K reabsorption/secretion depend on plasma K · H+ depends on pH

## 4.6 Organic substance secretion (late PT, active)
- OA- (weak acid) endogenous: bile salts, fatty acid, hippurates (PAH), hydroxybenzoates, oxalate, prostaglandin\*\*\*, urate\*\*\* · drugs: acetazolamide, chlorothiazide, ethacrynate, furosemide\*\*\*, penicillin\*\*\*, probenecid\*\*\*, salicylates · transporters NaDC, OAT, MRP, BCRP
  - interaction: probenecid ↔ penicillin or cidofovir (delay secretion) · NSAID ↔ methotrexate
- OC+ (weak base) endogenous: ACh, choline, **creatinine**, dopamine, E/NE, guanidine, histamine, serotonin, thiamine · drugs: atropine, isoproterenol, cimetidine, morphine, procaine, quinine · OCT, MDR, MATE
  - interaction: trimethoprim or cimetidine ↔ procainamide · pyrimethamine ↔ metformin

## 5. FE
- FE = % of filtered solute excreted = (UA×V)/(PA×GFR)×100 = (UA×Pcr)/(PA×Ucr)×100 = CA/CCr×100 (clearance ratio)
- FE 100% inulin, creatinine · <100% Na, Cl, K, Pi, urea · 0% glucose, AA · >100% PAH, organic acids/bases
- normal: FENa 1–2%, FEK 30–40%, FEurea 20–40% (ตามสไลด์), FEPi 15–20%
- FENa for AKI: pre-renal → ↑Na reabsorption → **FENa <1%** · ATN → **FENa >2%**

## 6. Concentrating & diluting
- urine osm 50 – 1,200/1,400 mOsm/kg · TAL + early DT dilute; late DT + CD concentrate (depend ADH + medullary osmolality)
- Volume expansion/DI → reabsorb solute without water → dilute · volume contraction/SIADH → concentrated
- Countercurrent multiplication (loop) sets gradient: ADH ↑NaCl reabsorption ↑gradient; loop diuretic ↓
- Countercurrent exchanger (vasa recta) maintains: volume overload or osmotic diuretic ↑vasa recta flow → washout ↓gradient
- Urea recycling: urea ~40–50% (500–600 mOsm) of inner medulla osmolality · high protein diet ↑ · dehydration: ADH ↑ water reabsorption → ↑tubular urea, ↑UT-A1 → ↑urea reabsorption · (figure %: 50% reabsorbed PT, 50% secreted thin limbs, 80% reabsorbed?, 25% accumulation, 5% vasa recta — ตัวเลขในภาพจับคู่ไม่ชัด)
- BUN 5–25 mg/dL · **BUN/Cr ratio 20:1** separates pre-renal (↑↑↑BUN/↑Cr) from ATN
- Factors elevating medullary gradient: ↑loop NaCl transport · ↑ADH · ↓GFR (slower flow) · ↓vasa recta flow · ↑protein diet

## 7. Cosm & CH2O
- Cosm = Uosm×V/Posm, normal ~3 mL/min · ↑: high protein diet, diuretic, DM · ↓: fasting, protein-deficient diet
- CH2O = V − Cosm, normal ~ −1 mL/min · free water generated in TAL + early DT (diluting segment), and late DT/CD when no ADH
  - Uosm = Posm → CH2O 0: loss of concentrating/diluting (prolonged loop diuretic, ATN)
  - Uosm > Posm (600/300) → negative: water deprivation, volume loss, SIADH
  - Uosm < Posm (100/300) → positive: water drinking, DI
- urine flow ~1–1.5 L/day · oliguria <400 mL/day · anuria <50 mL/day · diuresis >2 mL/min (>2.8 L/day) · polyuria >3 L/day
- water diuresis ↑↑↑CH2O ↔Cosm (water drinking, DI) · osmotic diuresis ↑↑↑Cosm ↓CH2O (diuretic, DM)
- ปิดด้วยภาพ "Summary of transportation of fluids through the urinary system" (ภาพ)
