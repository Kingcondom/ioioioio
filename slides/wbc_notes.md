## หน้า 1–4 · Leukocytosis
- p2 ค่าปกติ: **WBC 4,000–11,000 /mcL** · Neutrophil 40–60% · Lymphocyte 20–40% · Monocyte 2–8% · Eosinophil 1–4% · Basophil 0.5–1%
  - **Absolute cell count = % differential × total WBC** (Berliner, Cecil 24th 2012)
- p3 เกณฑ์ (George TI ASH 2012):
  | | |
  |---|---|
  | Leukocytosis | > 11,000 /mcL |
  | **Leukemoid reaction** | **> 50,000 /mcL** |
  | Neutrophilia | > 7,700 |
  | Lymphocytosis | > 3,500 |
  | Monocytosis | > 1,000 |
  | Eosinophilia | > 500 |
  | Hypereosinophilia | > 1,500 |
  | Basophilia | > 300 |
- p4 Cause of neutrophilia: กล่อง Infection & Inflammation · **Stress** (exercise, convulsions, pain, labor, anesthesia, surgery, cold, heat, burns, electric shock, trauma, infarction, emotional stress) · Drug · Malignancy · Miscellaneous

## หน้า 5–8 · Mechanism of neutrophilia (Williams 8th) — แผนภาพ pool
- p5 **Bone marrow pools**: Mitotic pool → Maturation pool → **Storage pool** (อยู่ในไขกระดูก "few days") → (few hours) → **Blood pools**: Marginal pool ⇄ Circulating pool → (few minutes) → Tissue
- p6 กลไก 3 แบบที่ทำให้นับได้สูงเร็ว:
  - **Marrow storage pool shift** (ปล่อยจากคลังในไขกระดูก): **Endotoxin, Corticosteroids**
  - **Marginal → circulating pool shift** (demargination): **Exercise, Adrenaline**
  - **Egress (ออกสู่เนื้อเยื่อ) ลดลง**: **Corticosteroids**
  - กล่องแดง: Acute stress · Intoxication · Hypoxia · Infection
- p7 **Proliferation ↑** (mitotic pool) · **↑ Peripheral granulocyte survival** · กล่องแดง: Infection, Inflammation, Malignancy, Endocrinopathy, Post-neutropenia · และ **CML**
- p8 Leukocytosis with **left shift** (ภาพ smear) — ✍️ "L → เจอตัวอ่อน" (พบเซลล์ตัวอ่อนในกระแสเลือด)

## หน้า 9–12 · Reactive vs CML
- p9 Toxic granulation (ภาพ) · p10 Cytoplasmic vacuolization (ภาพ)
- p11 **Leukemoid VS Leukemia** (Williams 8th)
  | Reactive neutrophilia / Leukemoid reaction | CML |
  |---|---|
  | Fever, splenomegaly: ขึ้นกับสาเหตุ reactive | **Unexplained splenomegaly** · Fever in advanced phase |
  | Myelocyte, metamyelocyte, increased band, **toxic granule** (✍️ infection), vacuolization | **Myelocyte**, promyelocyte, blast · **increased basophils, eosinophils** |
  | **LAP score: high** | **LAP score: low** |
  | Presence of reactive causes · DDx: **chronic neutrophilic leukemia (rare)** | **Philadelphia chromosome t(9;22) or BCR-ABL1: positive** |
  - ✍️ ไฮไลต์: toxic granule, myelocyte, increased basophils/eosinophils, LAP low, Ph/BCR-ABL1 · ขีดเส้นใต้ unexplained splenomegaly · เขียน "⇒ WBC" ข้าง CML
- p12 CML smear: **Normochromic normocytic RBC · Leukocytosis with multistage of myeloid cells · Increased eosinophil and basophil · Increased platelet**

## หน้า 13–16 · Causes: neutrophilia (รายละเอียด), monocytosis, lymphocytosis
- p13 Cause of neutrophilia (Williams 8th):
  - **Drug**: G-CSF, epinephrine, glucocorticoids, androgen, lithium, vaccines — ✍️ วงไว้ "ใช้ steroid"
  - **Malignancy**: solid cancer esp. advanced stage · lymphoma, MM · CML
  - **Miscellaneous**: pregnancy, preeclampsia, thyroid storm, overproduction of ACTH, smoking, venoms, ethylene glycol, post neutropenia, hemolysis or hemorrhage, post splenectomy, Down syndrome, congenital neutrophilia
- p14 Cause of **monocytosis**:
  - Reactive — Infection: **TB, syphilis, subacute BE, CMV, VZV, dengue, malaria** · Inflammation · Stress: surgery, trauma, pregnancy, labor, MI · Misc: postsplenectomy, post CMT, post BMT, corticosteroids, solid cancer, MM, lymphoma
  - **Myeloid neoplasms** (✍️ "BM abnormal"): **CMML, CML, atypical CML, JMML, AML M4/5**
- p15 Cause of **lymphocytosis**:
  - Reactive — Infection: **mononucleosis syndrome*** (EBV, CMV, VZV, HSV type 2, 6, 8, HIV, rubella, adenovirus, hepatitis virus, dengue, toxoplasmosis) · pertussis, brucellosis, cat-scratch disease, TB, leprosy, syphilis, rickettsiosis, toxoplasmosis, malaria, leishmaniasis, babesiosis · Drug reaction · Misc: post splenectomy, post CMT, post BMT, malignancy, thymoma
  - **Lymphoid neoplasms**
- p16 **Approach to lymphocytosis**: (1) Evidence of reactive cause: infection, drug → (2) Morphology: **blood smear — pleomorphic vs monomorphic** → (3) Immunophenotype: **flow cytometry** (✍️ ✓) · ✍️ "CLL > 5000" (เกณฑ์ monoclonal B lymphocyte ≥ 5,000/µL ของ iwCLL)

## หน้า 17–26 · ภาพ smear (lymphocytes)
- p17 **Infectious mononucleosis** (✍️ ✓) — atypical/reactive lymphocyte ไซโตพลาสซึมกว้าง ขอบเกาะ RBC
- p18 Pertussis — small lymphocytes, cleaved/clefted nuclei
- p19 Dengue fever — ✍️ "Plasmacytoid" (lymphocyte แบบ plasmacytoid)
- p20 **CLL — small mature lymphocyte · smudge cell** — ✍️ "หน้าเหมือนกันหมด" (monomorphic)
- p21 **ALL — lymphoblast, fine chromatin with nucleolus** ✓
- p22 **PLL — prolymphocyte, prominent nucleolus** ✓
- p23 Small lymphocytes with **cleaved nuclei** — mantle cell lymphoma, follicular lymphoma, marginal zone lymphoma
- p24 Small-to-medium lymphoid cells **with vacuolization** — **Burkitt leukemia/lymphoma** ✓
- p25 **Hairy cells — hairy cell leukemia** — ✍️ วง "ไข่ดาว" (fried-egg)
- p26 Villous lymphocyte — splenic lymphoma with villous lymphocyte

## หน้า 27–28 · Eosinophilia
- p27 (Gotlib 2011 · Klion 2015) **Eosinophilia** — AEC: **Mild 500–1,500 · Moderate 1,500–5,000 · Severe > 5,000 /µL** (✍️ "เจอบ่อย")
  - **Hypereosinophilia** AEC > 1,500 /µL (✍️ "EOD")
  - **Hypereosinophilic syndrome (HES)** = hypereosinophilia + **evidence of end-organ manifestations attributable to the eosinophilia**
- p28 Cause of eosinophilia (Williams 8th): Reactive — **Infection** · **Drug*** (✍️ วง) · **Allergic disease* (mild)** (✍️ Asthma) · Misc: CNT (connective tissue) disease, vasculitis, sarcoidosis, cancer, ABPA, eosinophilic pneumonia, eosinophilic gastroenteritis, **hypoadrenalism** · **Clonal eosinophilia** (✍️ "BM →") · **Hypereosinophilic syndrome**

## หน้า 29–36 · Leukopenia / Neutropenia
- p29 **Leukopenia < 4,000/mcL** · **Neutropenia < 1,500** · **Lymphopenia < 1,000** · Others (monocytopenia, eosinopenia, basopenia) — no clinical significance
- p30 Neutropenia: **physiological variation — confirm on ≥ 3 samples over several weeks** (Dale 2003)
  | Mild | ANC 1,000–1,500 |
  | Moderate | ANC 500–1,000 |
  | **Severe / Agranulocytosis** | **ANC < 500** |
  - **Risk for major pyogenic infections and life-threatening infections**
- p31 Classification (Boxer ASH 2012): **Acute: few days – 3 months · Chronic: > 3 months**
  - Mechanisms: **Impaired production · Increased destruction · Margination and sequestration** (แผนภาพ pool เดียวกับ p5)
- p32 Cause of neutropenia (Williams 8th): Infection · **Drug*** · Misc — **Immune: SLE, RA, Sjögren, Felty, LGL** · congenital neutropenia · margination · hypersplenism · idiopathic · **Bone marrow failure** · **Nutritional deficiency**
- p33 **Infection-induced neutropenia** (Andersohn 2007 อ้างในสไลด์):
  | Infection of granulopoietic cell | EBV, CMV, HIV, hepatitis virus, rubella, influenza |
  | Endothelial cell adherence or neutrophil utilization | babesiosis, rickettsiosis, dengue, measles, septicemia |
  | Splenomegaly | brucellosis, salmonellosis, tuberculosis, malaria, kala-azar |
- p34 **Drug-induced neutropenia — definite causality** (Andersohn Ann Intern Med 2007):
  Analgesics/NSAIDs (aminopyrine, diclofenac, diflunisal, **dipyrone**, ibuprofen) · antiarrhythmics (disopyramide, **procainamide**, quinidine) · anti-infectives (ampicillin, carbenicillin, cefotaxime, cefuroxime, flucytosine, fusidic acid, imipenem, nafcillin, oxacillin, penicillin G, quinine, ticarcillin) · anticonvulsant (phenytoin) · antineoplastic (amygdalin) · antirheumatic (infliximab, levamisole) · **antithyroid (propylthiouracil)** · CVS (clopidogrel, methyldopa, ramipril, spironolactone) · GI (cimetidine, metoclopramide) · psychotropic (chlorpromazine, **clozapine**, fluoxetine) · others (calcium dobesilate, mebhydrolin)
- p35 **Approach to neutropenia**: History (onset, infection, drug, autoimmune disease, nutritional deficiency, family history) · PE (lymph node, liver and spleen, skin/oral cavity/hair/nail/bone) · Investigations (CBC + blood smear · anti-HIV, serology, culture · ANA, RF, antineutrophil Ab · copper, ceruloplasmin, B12, folate · bone marrow study)
- p36 Agranulocytosis — BM: **granulocytic aplasia or hypoplasia** (Theml 2004)
- p37 Agranulocytosis — BM: **maturation arrest of granulocytic lineage** (Theml 2004) → ภาพ BM สองแบบ = กลไก production ↓ (aplasia) vs หยุดการเจริญ (maturation arrest/ภูมิคุ้มกันทำลายตัวที่โตแล้ว)
- p38 **Myelodysplastic syndrome** (ภาพ dysplasia 3 สาย — Cazzola Blood 2013): erythroid (megaloblastoid, multinuclearity, ring sideroblast) · megakaryocyte (micromegakaryocyte, separated nuclei) · granulocytic (**pseudo-Pelger**, abnormal nuclear shape, **hypogranulation**, myeloblasts) — ✍️ "AML" (MDS ลุกลามเป็น AML)

## หน้า 39–41 · Hyperleukocytosis
- p39 ✍️ "อาการ: stroke / confusion"
  - (ข้อความในสไลด์ที่เป็นของ hyperviscosity ทั่วไป) "CBC reveals elevated hematocrit and microscopic rouleau formation. The diagnosis of hyperviscosity comes with laboratory reporting that the blood is 'too thick' to run tests" · neurologic exam may reveal focal deficits
  - **Laboratory analysis is usually diagnostic**: **Common AML > ALL >>> CML, CLL**
    - AML WBC > 100,000 · "Acute myeloblastic leukemia WBC > 50,000–100,000" (สไลด์เขียนสองบรรทัดซ้อน — คงตามสไลด์)
    - **ALL WBC > 100,000–400,000**
    - **CML usually in accelerated/blastic phase, WBC > 300,000**
    - **CLL rarely seen, WBC > 500,000–1,000,000**
- p40 Clinical manifestation — **RS & Neuro** (ไฮไลต์):
  - RS/CVS: dyspnea, tachypnea, hypoxemia, **DAH**, CXR interstitial or alveolar infiltration
  - Neuro: headache, blurred vision, tinnitus, focal neurological deficit, **ICH**, AOC
  - Eye: retinal hemorrhage, papilledema, exudates, **sausage-like vessels** (ภาพ fundus + CXR)
- p41 Treatment
  - Supportive: **IV hydration, O2 therapy · observe TLS · Avoid PRC transfusion — it will worsen leukocytosis** (ไฮไลต์ · ✍️ "ยิ่งข้นนะ!")
  - Specific: specific chemotherapy · **Hydroxyurea 50–100 mg/kg/day in AML** · **Dexamethasone IV in ALL** (✍️ "สลาย lymphocyte") · **Leukapheresis: removes WBC but transient effect (contraindicated in APL → worsening DIC)** (✍️ "เอา WBC ออก")

## หน้า 42–46 · Lymphatic system & approach to lymphadenopathy
- p43 Primary lymphoid tissue: bone marrow, thymus · Secondary: peripheral lymph nodes, spleen, oropharyngeal lymphoid tissue incl. Waldeyer's ring, lymphoid aggregates in lamina propria/submucosa of respiratory & GI tract incl. Peyer's patches
- p44 Approach: **localized vs generalized lymphadenopathy** · Causes: **Infection: EBV, TB, NTM, fungus** · **Autoimmune: SLE, RA, Sjögren** · **Malignancy: lymphoma** · Rare: sarcoidosis, Castleman, Kikuchi, Rosai-Dorfman
- p45 Approach:
  - **Onset — Acute within 1–2 week(s): viral infection · Chronic: chronic infection, malignancy** (✍️ ลูกศรจาก 1–2 wk → "Augmentin")
  - **Size and characteristics**: **Lymphoma: varying size, rubbery firm, matted, and nontender** · **Metastatic cancer: varying size, hard, fixed with surrounding tissue** · **Infection: tender with erythematous skin, sinus tract formation** (✍️ วง rubbery firm, matted, nontender, hard, fixed)
  - Sites of enlarged lymph nodes →
- p46 ตาราง Region → Diseases:
  | Occipital | scalp infections, insect bites, ringworm, rarely lymphoma or metastatic tumors, cat-scratch disease |
  | Posterior auricular | rubella, oculoglandular syndromes |
  | Anterior auricular | infections of eyelids and conjunctiva, epidemic keratoconjunctivitis |
  | Posterior and anterior cervical | toxoplasmosis, adenovirus syndromes, infections of oral cavity and pharynx |
  | Posterior cervical and submental | scalp infections, dental infections, tuberculosis |
  | Cervical (suppurative) | tuberculosis |
  | **Supraclavicular (hard and fixed)** | **metastases from intrathoracic or intraabdominal carcinoma, Hodgkin's disease** |
  | Axillary | infection of upper extremities, cat-scratch disease, brucellosis, sporotrichosis, NHL |
  | Epitrochlear (unilateral) | infections of hands, Hodgkin's lymphoma |
  | Epitrochlear (bilateral) | viral disease in children, sarcoidosis, tularemia |
  | Inguinal (unilateral) | LGV, syphilis, chancroid, gonococcal, herpetic venereal infections, mycoplasma, urethritis, tularemia, plague, loiasis, onchocerciasis, lymphoma, metastatic pelvic carcinoma, gynecologic malignancies |
  | Inguinal (bilateral) | lymphoma |
  | Hilar (unilateral) | metastatic lung carcinoma |
  | Hilar (bilateral) | sarcoidosis, tuberculosis, histoplasmosis, coccidioidomycosis |
  | Mediastinal, symmetric | lymphoma |
  | Intraabdominal and retroperitoneal | lymphoma, metastatic carcinoma, TB in mesenteric nodes |
  | Regional involvement in systemic infections | infectious mononucleosis, viral hepatitis, CMV, rubella, influenza, leprosy, toxoplasmosis, AIDS |
  | Generalized lymphadenopathy | sarcoidosis, hyperthyroidism, SLE, lymphoma, syphilis |

## หน้า 47–57 · Lymphoma
- p47 Lymphoma: neoplasm of cells derived from lymphocytes or precursors · originates from lymphoid cells at different stages of maturation · manifests primarily as tumour of lymph nodes or related organs (spleen, liver, other solid tissue)
- p48–49 แผนภาพ B-cell development: BM (progenitor → pre-B → immature B) → naive peripheral B → follicle (centroblast → centrocyte; FDC; mantle cell rim) → memory B / long-lived plasma cell; extrafollicular B-blast → short-lived plasma cell; marginal zone · p50 T-cell: common lymphoid progenitor → precursor T → thymus (thymocyte CD4/CD8 selection ~2% survive) → αβ / γδ T cell → peripheral T-cell repertoire
- p51 **Hodgkin lymphoma**: usually arises in lymph nodes, **especially cervical** · **mostly young adult** · **small number of scattered large mononucleated and multinucleated tumour cells** (ภาพ Reed–Sternberg) · **B symptoms in 1/3** · **emergency: venous obstruction, airway obstruction**
- p52 **Indolent NHL** (✍️ "ค่อยๆโต"): follicular lymphoma · marginal zone lymphoma · CLL/SLL · Common manifestation: **lymphadenopathy/mass effect (usually generalized)** · **immune cytopenia** · **absolute lymphocytosis** · **hyperviscosity syndrome**
- p53 Immune cytopenia: **AIHA** (smear spherocytes, polychromasia) · **ITP** · petechiae · bleeding per gum (ภาพ)
- p54 Classification (WHO, ภาพ): HL = **classical HL (NS, MC, LR, LD)** + **NLPHL** · NHL: **Indolent** (B: follicular, SLL/CLL, marginal zone, LP (WM) · T/NK: mycosis fungoides, Sézary syndrome, primary cutaneous ALCL) · **Aggressive** (B: DLBCL, FL grade 3 and tFL, mantle cell, primary effusion · T/NK: ALCL, angioimmunoblastic, subcutaneous panniculitis-like, blastic NK, extranodal NK/T nasal, enteropathy-type, hepatosplenic, PTCL NOS) · **Highly aggressive** (B: pre-B lymphoblastic, Burkitt · T: pre-T lymphoblastic)
- p55 Aggressive NHL — ภาพคลินิก: ก้อน/ผื่นที่ผิวหนัง, ก้อนที่เบ้าตา (CT), ใบหน้าบวม/ก้อนจมูก (เข้าได้กับ extranodal NK/T nasal), แผล/ก้อนที่หนังศีรษะ
- p56 Aggressive NHL — ภาพ DVT (venous obstruction) + ขาบวม
- p57 **Very aggressive NHL — Burkitt cells** (vacuolated) · **Endemic or African Burkitt** (ก้อนที่ขากรรไกร)

## หน้า 58–64 · Complications / oncologic emergencies of lymphoma
- p58 Complication: **compressive symptoms — cord compression, airway obstruction** · **Tumour lysis syndrome**: hyperkalemia, hyperphosphatemia, hyperuricemia, lactic acidosis, rising creatinine, hypocalcemia
- p59 TLS: **occurs spontaneously or after CMT** · Disease risk — tumour type: **AML (WBC > 50,000) · ALL (WBC > 100,000) · aggressive-type lymphoma** · **bulky disease (> 10 cm)** · **LDH > 2 times of UNL**
- p60 **Cairo–Bishop** (Cairo & Bishop 2004; Coiffier 2008; Howard 2011):
  - **Laboratory TLS: ≥ 2 abnormalities within 3 days before or 7 days after treatment** — **K ≥ 6.0 mEq/L · uric acid ≥ 8.0 mg/dL · phosphate ≥ 4.5 mg/dL · Ca ≤ 7.0 mg/dL** (หรือเปลี่ยน 25% จาก baseline)
  - **Clinical TLS = LTLS + AKI (Cr ↑ 0.3 mg/dL above baseline, oliguria < 0.5 mL/kg/h for 6 h, or Cr ≥ 1.5 ULN) / seizures, neuromuscular irritability / cardiac arrhythmia or sudden death**
  - กล่องไทยในสไลด์: "การวินิจฉัยจะใช้ Clinical ร่วมกับ Lab ที่เข้าได้ โดยใช้ Cairo-Bishop criteria ซึ่งเอา clinical TLS (CTLS) ≥ 1 ข้อ + Lab TLS (LTLS) ≥ 2 ข้อ" · ✍️ "PKCU"
- p61 TLS: Lab test — elevated Cr, low Ca, high P, acidosis and coagulopathy · Treatment:
  - **Allopurinol 300 mg twice daily for 3 days → once daily**
  - **Hydration with NSS 2000 mL/d**
  - **Rasburicase 0.15 mg/kg IV daily up to 5 days** if present TLS or rising Cr or hyperuricemia
  - **Hemodialysis** in refractory case, hyperkalemia, volume overload if oliguric
  - **Uric acid → best predictor for recovery of TLS**
- p62 **SVC syndrome** sign & symptoms: (I) early findings — **neck and upper thoracic venous distention, facial plethora and telangiectasia, mild edema of face and arms** (II) **palpable supraclavicular mass** may be identified (III) **CXR often shows mediastinal or lung abnormality** (ภาพ: edematous head and neck with naso/oropharyngeal edema, collateral chest wall veins, swelling of upper arms/torso, cyanotic skin)
- p63 **DDx of mediastinal lymphoma**:
  | Lymphoma | Sex | Age | Characteristics |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Classical HL | M = F | young adults | usually nodular sclerosis subtype |
  | T-ALL | male | AYA | BM failure |
  | DLBCL | M = F | older adults | usually involves additional distant nodal or extranodal sites |
  | PMBCL | female | AYA | disease limited to the mediastinum |
- p64 SVC treatment: **corticosteroid IV (eg. dexamethasone 5 mg IV q 6 h) in case with suspected lymphoma** (✍️ "ยุบแน่นอน" → "ต้องทำหลัง get tissue!") · **furosemide and to reduce the ICP** · **consult RT — definitive mediastinal radiotherapy** · (ภาพ CXR mediastinal widening)

## หน้า 65–66 · Ann Arbor staging (ภาพ)
- Stage I: single lymph node region or single extralymphatic site (IE) · II: ≥ 2 node regions same side of diaphragm (may include localized extralymphatic, IIE) · III: node regions both sides of diaphragm (may include spleen IIIS or localized extranodal IIIE) · IV: **diffuse extralymphatic disease** (liver, bone marrow, lung, skin) (✍️ วง extralymphatic)
- NB: **unexplained weight loss > 10% in preceding 6 months and/or fevers > 38 °C and night sweats → 'B'; if absent 'A'**
- p66 Thank you
