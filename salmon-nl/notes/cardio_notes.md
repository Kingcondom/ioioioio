## Key facts
ACLS: PEA = any EKG no pulse not VF/VT/asystole. Non-shockable (asystole/PEA) -> epinephrine ASAP + CPR. Pulseless VT after adrenaline x3 -> defibrillation (S12-13). Amiodarone/lidocaine 1st dose after 3rd shock, 2nd dose after 5th shock (3-5 min after 1st). Old Qs: lightning asystole S14; PEA S16-19 (epi 1 mg IV).
Tachy list S22: regular narrow SVT/flutter; irregular narrow AF/flutter/MAT; regular wide mono VT; irregular wide poly VT, AF+WPW. SVT can't see P; flutter sawtooth; MAT >=3 P morphologies; AF+WPW delta wave.
SVT stable: 1 vagal 2 adenosine 3 BB/non-DHP CCB (S40,42). Unstable = hypotension, AOC, shock, chest discomfort, AHF -> synchronized cardioversion (S43-48).
AF S25: palpitation, dyspnea, syncope, irregularly irregular; RVR HR>100. Complication AHF, stroke/TIA, renal/splenic infarct, intestinal ischemia, ALI. Unstable -> sync cardioversion (anticoag before). Stable rate: 1st BB (esmolol, metoprolol, propranolol, atenolol) / non-DHP (diltiazem, verapamil); 2nd digoxin, amiodarone; goal <110 (<80 if still sx). Rhythm: electrical/pharm (flecainide, propafenone, amiodarone) + pericardioversion anticoag. Anticoag: mod-severe MS, mechanical valve, HCM -> warfarin; else CHA2DS2-VASc >=1 men, >=2 women -> DOAC/warfarin. Q S35 stable AF 122 -> atenolol; S37 AF+MS -> oral anticoagulant.
Brady S51-71: SND (sinus brady <60, sinus pause/arrest >3 s P-P not multiple, SA block P-P multiple, tachy-brady), AV blocks. Mobitz II / CHB: unstable -> atropine, pacing; stable -> pacing. Q S62/64 sinus pause -> sick sinus syndrome. S66 PR 30 BP 90/60 fatigue -> atropine.
VHD S74-78: AS degenerative calcification (most common), bicuspid, RF; crescendo-decrescendo SEM R 2nd ICS radiate carotid. AR acute IE/dissection/trauma; chronic bicuspid, RF, Marfan; diastolic blowing L 3rd ICS (Erb). MS rheumatic; diastolic rumbling apex + opening snap. MR degenerative, DCM, Marfan, MI, RF, IE; systolic apex -> axilla. PS congenital; SEM L2 wide split S2 radiate back. PR pulm HT, DCM; diastolic L2. TS RF, IE; diastolic L4. TR RV dilation, IE, RF, Marfan; systolic L4. Qs S79-94 (MS, MR, MR, RHD, AS, AS, AS, AS->AVR).
Syncope S95-115: prodrome vasovagal nausea diaphoresis pallor; orthostatic dizziness; cardiac palpitation. Rapid, short (<1 min), complete recovery ± brief myoclonic jerk. Mimics: seizure (tongue bite lateral, incontinence, postictal), brainstem TIA, subclavian steal, hypoglycemia. Reflex (vasovagal emotional; situational cough/swallow/micturition/defecation; carotid sinus shaving/necktie). Orthostatic: hypovolemia, drugs, autonomic failure (PD, DM); SBP drop >=20 or DBP >=10. Cardiac: arrhythmia, structural (severe AS, HOCM, massive MI, mass, tamponade), dissection, PE, pulm HT; syncope supine/exertion, FHx SCD, abnormal EKG. Ix: reflex/orthostatic clinical, tilt table if unclear; arrhythmia -> Holter; structural -> echo.
HT S116-152: HMOD vascular/heart/kidney/brain/eye. Start drug office >=140/90; >=130/80 if clinical CVD, DM, Thai CV risk >=10%. 5 main classes ACEI/ARB, BB, CCB, thiazide; no ACEI+ARB. Usually start 2: (ACEI/ARB)+(CCB or thiazide); step 2 triple. Comorbidity table S129-131: stable CAD ACEI/ARB, BB; angina BB, DHP, non-DHP (avoid BB+non-DHP); HFrEF ACEI/ARB/ARNI+BB+MRA+SGLT2i (avoid non-DHP); diuretic thiazide vs loop severe edema; CKD UACR>=30 ACEI/ARB; stroke ACEI/ARB+diuretic; DM UACR>=30 ACEI/ARB (finerenone if max); DM+ASCVD SGLT2i/GLP1RA; DM+HF/CKD GFR<60 SGLT2i/GLP1RA; AS ACEI/ARB, BB; AR ACEI/ARB; obesity ACEI/ARB; OSA MRA, ACEI/ARB; AF BB, ACEI/ARB (avoid BB+non-DHP). Resistant = 3 drugs max tolerated. Qs: S134 first high office BP -> HBPM/ABPM or repeat (answer lifestyle); S136 DM proteinuria -> enalapril; S138 enalapril; S140 enalapril; S142 enalapril (1 drug ok in advanced age); S144 DM+COPD -> amlodipine (avoid BB bronchospasm, ACEI cough); S146 on enalapril -> add amlodipine (not losartan); S148 masked HT -> LSM, drug if high CVD risk/HMOD; S150 -> home BP measurement; S152 CCB edema -> discontinue amlodipine.
Secondary HT S153: age <40, resistant, emergency, exacerbation, TOD disproportionate, unprovoked hypoK. RAS: athero, FMD, flash pulmonary edema, abdominal bruit, Cr rise >=50% within 1 wk of ACEI/ARB, hypoK, asymmetric kidneys; Ix duplex/MRA/CTA. ADPKD: renal US. Conn: resistant, incidentaloma, hypoK met alk; PAC up, PRA down, ARR up; correct hypoK first. Pheo: paroxysmal headache palpitation diaphoresis; 24h urine fractionated metanephrines/plasma metanephrines. Coarctation: arm-leg BP difference; echo. OSA: sleep study. ACEI Cr rise <=30% continue; >30% stop + find cause (S163). Qs S161 RAS, S165 Doppler, S167 GN, S169 RFT, S171 pheo, S173 primary aldo (ans? K 3 on OCP), S175 serum electrolytes, S177 electrolytes/PAC/PRA.
Crises S178-189: emergency >=180/110 + end-organ damage; urgency no EOD. Malignant HT: bilateral retinopathy, AKI, MAHA (any). Emergency: IV nicardipine, labetalol, nitroprusside (avoid nitroprusside in renal/liver impairment). Urgency: oral, lower over 24-48 h, F/U 2-4 wk. LVH/S4 without pulmonary edema not acute EOD.
Pericarditis S190-194: causes virus (most common), bacteria, MI, postop, uremia, radiation, malignancy, trauma, idiopathic. Pleuritic pain better leaning forward, rub, low fever; EKG diffuse STE + PR depression, later TWI. Constrictive: fluid overload, JVP up, Kussmaul, hepatomegaly, HJR, edema, pericardial knock, pulsus paradoxus, echo pericardial thickness. Pulsus paradoxus SBP drop >10 on inspiration. Mx NSAID/ASA + colchicine; prednisolone severe; pericardiocentesis tamponade/large; pericardiectomy constrictive persistent; TB pericarditis -> prednisolone to prevent constriction.
Effusion S195-198: hemopericardium (wall rupture, trauma, dissection, surgery); serous (idiopathic, pericarditis, malignancy, postpericardiotomy, uremia, autoimmune). Echo gold std. EKG low voltage <5 mm limb, <10 mm precordial; electrical alternans. CXR flask-shaped, no pulmonary edema.
Tamponade S199: Beck triad hypoTN, muffled HS, JVP up; dyspnea, tachycardia, pulsus paradoxus, R HF signs, lung clear. Tx pericardiocentesis, IV fluid cautiously.
Dissection S218-230: HT most common; trauma, CTD. Tearing pain to back, BP asym (>=10 inter-arm abnormal). Complications AR, MI, tamponade, stroke, AKI, paraplegia. EKG r/o STEMI, CXR widened mediastinum, CTA C/A/P gold std; echo if unstable/renal failure. Keep SBP 100-120, HR <=60: 1st IV BB (labetalol, esmolol), 2nd vasodilator (nicardipine, NTG) prevent reflex tachy; morphine; surgery Stanford A or B complicated.
CAD S231-239: stable angina features; Ix EKG; low PTP no test ± CAC/exercise; intermediate-high CCTA or stress; abnormal -> CAG. Mx ASA, high-intensity statin, comorbidity; BB 1st, CCB/nitrate 2nd; revasc PCI/CABG high risk. Q S237 CCTA; S239 atenolol.
ACS S240-258: STEMI complete occlusion transmural; NSTEMI biomarker up subendocardial; UA no biomarker. EKG 10 min. STE >=1 mm 2 contiguous (V2-3 >=2 mm men, >=1.5 women) or new LBBB. ST dep V1-4 -> V7-9 posterior; inferior -> V3R-V6R. Territory table S246: V1-2 septal LAD; V2-4 anterior LAD; V5-6 I aVL lateral LCX(,LAD); II III aVF inferior RCA(,LCX); V7-9 posterior RCA/LCX; V3R-V4R RV RCA. hs-cTn 0/1h; 0/3h: 0h>ULN -> 3h change >20%; 0h<ULN -> change >50%; ULN 99th pct. STEMI: PCI if <=120 min else fibrinolysis (tenecteplase, alteplase, reteplase). PCI: DAPT ASA + clopidogrel/prasugrel/ticagrelor + heparin. Lysis: ASA + clopidogrel + heparin/enoxaparin/fondaparinux. NSTE: GRACE, TIMI; no fibrinolytic; DAPT + heparin/enox/fonda. Adjunct: O2 if SpO2 <90, nitrates (not in RV infarct), high-intensity statin, BB, ACEI/ARB. Bleeding risk -> shorten DAPT.
