## AKI (7–17, 25–47)
- Def ข้อใดข้อหนึ่ง: ↑Cr ≥0.3 ใน 48 hr · ↑Cr ≥1.5x baseline ใน 7 วัน · UO <0.5 ml/kg/hr ≥6 hr
- Broad cast → CKD
- Prerenal: hypovolemia (hemorrhage, V/D, burn, diuretic), ↓BP (sepsis, cardiogenic, anaphylaxis), ↓ECV (cardiorenal, hepatorenal), drugs NSAID ACEI ARB CNI
- Renal: ATN ischemic/nephrotoxic (contrast, AG, cisplatin, ampho B, tenofovir, Hb, myoglobin); AIN (penicillin, PPI, NSAID; infection; infiltrative lymphoma sarcoid amyloid); glomerulus/microvascular (GN, TTP/HUS, preeclampsia); large vessel (bilat RAS, RA/RV thrombosis, vasculitis)
- Postrenal: bilat ureteral obstruction (stone tumor clot), bladder neck (stone tumor BPH), neurogenic bladder, urethral (PUV, tumor, stricture, phimosis), drugs (opioid, sympathomimetic, anticholinergic)

- ตาราง (16): BUN/Cr >20 / <15 / vary; FENa <1% / >1–2%; FEUrea <35% / >50%; UNa <20 / >40; Uosm >500 / <350 / <350; sediment hyaline / muddy brown ATN, RBC cast GN, fatty NS, WBC cast AIN / hematuria
- ATN (18–19): ischemic; nephrotoxic exogenous (contrast, AG, cisplatin, MTX, amphoB, tenofovir), endogenous (Hb, myoglobin, uric acid TLS, Ig MM); UA muddy brown granular cast, RTE cells, blood+; BUN/Cr <15, FENa >2%; Tx supportive

## CKD (142–156)
- causes DN, HT, GN, other (obstructive, analgesic, PKD, amyloid)
- def >3 mo: GFR <60 or damage (UACR ≥30, sediment, lytes, imaging, biopsy, transplant)
- ESRD = GFR <15 + RRT (ตาราง stage เป็นภาพ)
- symptoms late: nocturia oliguria, hematuria foamy, HT edema, fatigue N/V pruritus, pulmonary edema, uremic pericarditis/pleuritis, encephalopathy, anemia, bleeding (↓platelet adhesion)
- Ix BUN Cr CBC lytes UA UPCR UACR KUB U/S; DM FBS HbA1c; GN ANA ANCA anti-GBM complement; SPEP; biopsy if rapid ↓GFR / confirm GN
- Mx: cause; slow progression ACEI/ARB SGLT2i low protein low Na LSM avoid nephrotoxin; ASCVD HT ACEI/ARB, DM metformin SGLT2i, statin; acidosis NaHCO3 if HCO3 <22 keep 22–26; anemia correct IDA, ESA if Hb <10 keep 11–12; MBD ↑PO4 ↓Ca ↑PTH ↓vitD → low PO4 diet, binder Ca (CaCO3) / non-Ca (sevelamer, lanthanum, Al(OH)3), vit D, keep normal avoid hyperCa; hyperK low K diet; volume Na fluid restriction diuretics; vaccine HBV influenza COVID pneumococcal; malignancy screen; RRT counsel from CKD 4; HD PD KT when failed medical: volume/HT, acidosis, hyperK, uremic pericarditis, encephalopathy

## ABG (208–259)
- normal pH 7.35–7.45, PaO2 80–100, PaCO2 35–45, HCO3 22–26
- ABG vs VBG: calculated SpO2 ~ pulse ox; SpO2 95 = PaO2 80; 90 = 60; 88 = 55
- oxygenation: mild 60–80, mod 40–60, severe <40; A-a normal 2.5 + age/4; PF ratio ~500
- ventilation: <35 hyperventilation, >45 hypoventilation
- steps: pH; PaCO2 same direction = metabolic, opposite = respiratory; compensation: MA predicted CO2 = 1.5 HCO3 + 8 ±2; MAlk ΔCO2 = 0.7 ΔHCO3 ±2 (pred = 0.7ΔHCO3 + 40); resp per 10 CO2: acute acid 1, acute alk 2, chronic acid 4, chronic alk 5; trick pH decimals ≈ PaCO2 (metabolic only)
- AG = Na − Cl − HCO3 normal 10 ±2; corrected AG = AG + 2.5(4 − alb) ใช้เมื่อ AG ปกติ; delta ratio <1 + NAGMA, 1–2 pure, >2 + met alk
