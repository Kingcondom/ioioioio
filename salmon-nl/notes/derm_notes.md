## Eczema (7–47)
- Acute: erythema edema vesicle oozing · subacute: erythema scale crust · chronic: thick pigmented, lichenification (7–10)
- Exogenous: irritant, allergic · Endogenous: AD, SD, nummular, dyshidrotic, stasis (+ asteatotic, pityriasis alba, LSC หน้า 20)
- ICD (11–13): acute = single strong irritant (acid/alkali, Paederus "kissing lesion", jellyfish) ทุกคนเป็น, limited to exposure area, seconds–hrs; chronic cumulative = water soap detergent, gradual. Jellyfish: acetic acid/น้ำส้มสายชู/น้ำทะเล ห้ามน้ำจืด/alcohol; เอาหนวดออก (ถุงมือหนา แหนบ บัตรขูด). Mx avoid, PPE; acute irrigate water + wet dressing; chronic moisturizer + topical steroid
- ACD (14–19): type IV, repeated contact; nickel (ต่างหู นาฬิกา หัวเข็มขัด กระดุม แว่น), perfume/soap/cosmetics, gloves; เฉพาะบางคน; extend beyond area; rash 12–48 hr; patch test เมื่อไม่แน่ใจ/ไม่รู้เหตุ; avoid, moisturizer, wet dressing, topical steroid, prednisone; patch test แยก ACD/ICD. Q18 eye shadow → ACD
- AD (21–29): (slide: type I HS), triggers dry/humid heat, wool detergent, mite pollen fur, stress, food (egg milk peanut wheat); atopy hx; early childhood; AR asthma conjunctivitis; pruritus dry skin; infant face + extensor; child/adult flexor; ดีขึ้นเมื่อโต. Mx avoid trigger; mild moisturizer urea topical steroid; mod-severe TCI, phototherapy, immunosuppressant; Ix เมื่อไม่ดีขึ้น: IgE (severity), skin prick (trigger), patch (R/O contact). Qs 24 (5 mo cheeks oozing → AD), 26 (4 mo face forearms thighs scalp → AD), 28 (25 yo flexural → AD)
- SD (30–35): Malassezia, active sebaceous, Parkinson HIV; trigger stress sleep climate; erythematous patch/plaque greasy scale on scalp face eyebrow nasolabial retroauricular intertriginous; infantile cradle cap; dandruff. Tx topical antifungal (keto, ciclopirox), topical steroid; scalp shampoo keto/selenium/zinc pyrithione/tar; infant baby shampoo/oil แปรงเบาๆ หายเองเมื่อ 1 ขวบ. Q32 → SD; Q34 scalp greasy scale → topical ketoconazole
- Dyshidrotic (36–40): acute recurrent bilateral deep-seated vesicle/bullae palms soles lateral fingers, pruritus; Tx topical steroid, oral steroid severe. Q37 → dyshidrotic; Q39 → topical triamcinolone
- Stasis (41–43): chronic venous HT, medial malleolus, edema varicose ulcer; Tx topical steroid moisturizer + CVI (elevation, stocking). Q42 → static eczema
- Asteatotic (44) fine scale crack fissure · Nummular (45) discoid · Pityriasis alba (46) ill-defined hypopigmented; แยก PV ด้วย KOH, vitiligo ขอบชัด Wood lamp · LSC (47) plaque lichenification

## Urticaria (81–91)
- Type I, mast cell histamine; acute: food drugs infection insect; wheal & flare, pruritus, blanchable, resolve <24 hr, no residual pigment, ± angioedema; acute ≤6 wk, chronic >6 wk (≥2 ครั้ง/wk); stridor; dermographism
- Acute Mx: R/O anaphylaxis (criteria 3 ข้อ) → airway, IV fluid, IM epinephrine 1:1000 adult 0.3–0.5 mg, child 0.01 mg/kg; 2nd gen AH (cetirizine loratadine fexofenadine); add prednisolone severe; avoid trigger
- Chronic: tx cause; step1 2nd gen AH (ปรับได้ถึง 4 เท่า); step2 + omalizumab; step3 + cyclosporine; short pred severe exacerbation; acute self-limited ไม่ต้องหา cause; chronic ต้องหา
- Qs 86 seafood → acute urticaria; 88 migrating wheal → (hint) 2nd gen AH (choices epinephrine/hydroxyzine/steroid → hydroxyzine = antihistamine); 90 SLE 6 mo → chronic urticaria

## ADR (92–117)
- Type A dose-related predictable; type B not, hypersensitivity I–IV
- Simple: MPE, urticaria · Severe: fever, malaise, facial swelling, LN, blister erosion Nikolsky+, pustules, mucosa, eos/atypical lymph, LFT, Cr → SJS/TEN, DRESS, AGEP, exfoliative dermatitis
- MPE (99): ATB NSAID allopurinol AED anti-TB ARV; onset 7–14 d (2–5 d ถ้าเคยได้); Mx stop, topical steroid, oral/IV ถ้ากว้าง
- FDE (100–102): sulfonamide NSAID antihistamine pseudoephedrine AED; onset 1–2 wk (<48 hr re-exposure); well-defined round/oval red/violaceous patch ± central blister/necrosis; same site; PIH; stop + topical steroid. Q101 antibiotic → FDE
- SJS/TEN (103–115): <10% SJS, 10–30% overlap, >30% TEN; drugs most; HLA-B1502 CBZ lamotrigine phenytoin; B5801 allopurinol; B5701 abacavir; drugs allopurinol NSAID ATB nevirapine AED; onset 4–28 d (<48 hr re-exp); prodrome 1–3 d; painful dusky red/purpuric atypical target; Nikolsky+; bullae sloughing; mucosa ≥2; Mx stop, ICU/burn unit, IV fluid pain wound, consult eye gyne uro, monitor sepsis, hypovolemic shock. Qs 108 → SJS; 110 >30% → TEN; 112 lamotrigine >30% → TEN; 114 cotrimoxazole → prompt withdrawal
- DRESS (116): AED sulfonamide allopurinol nevirapine abacavir; 2–6 wk; fever facial swelling LN, generalized MP rash, organ; eos atypical lymph LFT Cr; Mx stop, prednisolone 3–6 mo + topical
- AGEP (117): ATB, omeprazole, CCB; 1–4 d; superficial pustules intertriginous → generalized; fever LN; neutrophil, LFT, Cr; stop + topical/oral steroid

## Blister / EM / AIBD (118–137)
- Localized: eczema, HSV, HZ, HFMD, bullous impetigo, NF, FDE, trauma · Generalized: severe eczema, skin necrosis (EM, SJS/TEN), AIBD (flaccid pemphigus, tense pemphigoid), infection (disseminated HSV/HZ, chickenpox, SSSS)
- EM (120–127): HSV most, mycoplasma, drugs (NSAID sulfa AED), SLE, idiopathic; target lesion (dusky center) hands feet → body; mucosa 1 site; Nikolsky −; EM major extensive + blister. Mx cause (acyclovir, stop drug); minor self-limited topical steroid AH; major systemic steroid; recurrent HSV-EM acyclovir/valacyclovir 6 mo. Qs 122 → EM; 124 recurrent lip + target → EM; 126 OTC drug target palms soles → EM (hint list cause)
- AIBD (128): PV anti-desmoglein intraepidermal flaccid; BP anti-hemidesmosome subepidermal tense
- PV (130): 50–60 yr, chronic oral ulcer first, flaccid blister erosion, Nikolsky +; biopsy intraepidermal acantholysis; DIF IgG reticular (intercellular) ; Tx prednisolone, rituximab, wound care
- BP (131): >60 yr, tense bullae pruritus ± urticaria, oral 10–30%, Nikolsky −; biopsy subepidermal eos; DIF linear IgG C3 DEJ; Tx topical steroid, pred severe, wound care
- Qs 132 → PV; 134 → BP; 136 tense bullae 70 yo → skin biopsy DIF + histopath (Tzanck ในตัวเลือก; เฉลย hint biopsy DIF)
