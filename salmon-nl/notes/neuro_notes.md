## Stroke (26–82)
- Ischemic 80% (TIA) · Hemorrhagic 20% (ICH, SAH)
- ACA: contralat LE>UE, incontinence · MCA: face&UE>LE, aphasia (dominant) · PCA: contralat homonymous hemianopia macular sparing
- Wallenberg (PICA/VA): ipsi dysphagia ↓gag hoarseness, ataxia, vertigo N/V nystagmus, Horner, face pain/temp loss; contra trunk/limb pain/temp

## Seizure & epilepsy (83–131)
- Trigger: fever, sleep deprivation, alcohol, flashing light, noise, stress, menses (trigger in known epilepsy)
- Causes systemic: hypo/hyperglycemia, Na, Ca, febrile; CNS: alcohol withdrawal, amphetamine, eclampsia, TBI, meningitis, encephalitis, stroke, uremic/hepatic, autoimmune encephalitis
- Aura only partial; ictal motor/nonmotor; postictal: drowsy, confusion, headache, Todd's paralysis, aphasia
- SE: ≥5 min or incomplete recovery between ≥2 seizures
- 0–5 min self-limited; 5–10 early SE: ABC, correct hypoglycemia, IV diazepam / IM midazolam, repeat 5–10 min; 10–30 established: IV phenytoin, phenobarbital, VPA, LEV, lacosamide; 40–60 refractory: ETT ICU cEEG, 2nd AED or anesthetics midazolam propofol thiopental ketamine
- Epilepsy: 2 unprovoked seizures >24 hr apart → start AED; step: seizure type → epilepsy → syndrome
- AED: focal CBZ PHT VPA PB; generalized VPA; pregnancy/young woman LEV LTG, avoid CBZ PHT VPA PB
- Stop AED: seizure-free ≥2 yr + normal exam & imaging · resolved: seizure-free 10 yr, off AED 5 yr
- S/E: VPA tremor alopecia pancreatitis wt gain terato; CBZ SIADH agranulocytosis terato ataxia hepatotox SJS; PHT hirsutism gingival hyperplasia nystagmus SJS drug-SLE DRESS sedation diplopia ataxia arrhythmia terato; PB cardiorespiratory depression sedation SJS; BZD sedation dependence resp depression
