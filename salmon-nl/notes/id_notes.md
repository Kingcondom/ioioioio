## Key facts
AUF: fever <2 wk w/o localizing sign; causes rickettsia (murine, scrub), lepto, typhoid, bacteremia, dengue, zika, chik, malaria. Ix CBC BUN Cr LFT UA; specific PBS, H/C, NS1, malaria RDT, serology dengue/scrub/lepto.
Melioid: B. pseudomallei soil/water; inhal/ingest; DM, thal, alcohol, liver/renal, immunosupp; Isan, farmer. Pneumonia most common; bacteremia; abscess liver spleen LN skin; pyelo; parotitis (kids); osteo/septic arthritis. Culture gold std; GNB bipolar safety pin; serology low spec; repeat culture. Tx drain; intensive IV ceftazidime/meropenem 10–14 d; eradication TMP/SMX 3–6 mo. Liver: Swiss cheese / cartwheel; splenic abscess → think melioid; ATB alone. Qs S11 melioid; S13 B.pseudomallei; S15 B.pseudomallei.
Lepto: L. interrogans, rodent urine via broken skin/mucosa. Anicteric: fever HA myalgia calf & low back, conjunctival suffusion, mild jaundice, aseptic meningitis, LN, HSM, rash. Weil: MOF liver failure, AKI, pulm hemorrhage, purpura ↓plt, ↓BP, myocarditis. Ix ↑WBC ↓plt ↑Cr AST ALT TB; dark-field, PCR, culture; MAT gold std 4-fold (80→320); blood/CSF in first 10 d, then urine. Tx mild oral doxy/amox/azithro; severe IV PGS, ceftriaxone, IV doxy. Qs S22 lepto; S24 (?lepto: TB 15, plt 80k) ; S26 MAT; S28 lepto titer (Widal not rec; Weil-Felix not rec); S30 Albumin 4/Glob 2.5 → lepto? (likely lepto vs melioid — answer image).
Rickettsia: scrub O. tsutsugamushi chigger mite (forest, waterfall, bushes) eschar yes; murine R. typhi rat flea (urban) no eschar; SFG tick eschar yes except RMSF; epidemic typhus R. prowazekii (lice). Fever severe HA, MP rash, relative bradycardia, LN (scrub), eschar axilla groin under breast; MOF severe. Ix ↓/↑WBC ↓plt; IFA gold 4-fold; Weil-Felix obsolete. Tx mild doxy 1st line; azithro preg/child <8; severe IV doxy/azithro/chloramphenicol; improves in 2–3 d (actually "ได้ยา 2-3 [วัน] ดีขึ้นชัด"). Eschar scrub small axilla/groin vs anthrax large edema hands/face. Qs S40 chigger; S42 IFA scrub; S44 rickettsia titer; S46 ELISA scrub IgG(?); S48 doxy; S50 doxy (scrub+lepto overlap?); S52 azithro (failed amox/clav → atypical).
Dengue: DEN1–4 Aedes; secondary heterotypic severe. DHF I tourniquet+, II spontaneous bleed; DSS III measurable BP narrow, IV unmeasurable. Ix WBC ≤5000 atypical lymph; Hct ↑5–10%; plt ≤150k; leakage Hct ↑≥20%, CXR effusion, U/S ascites GB wall, albumin ≤3.5 (≤4 obese). NS1 ≤5 d; IgM/IgG >5 d. Phases febrile/critical/recovery; WBC drop + ↑lymph + ↓plt → defervescence within 24 h. Febrile: OPD, PCM avoid NSAID, fluids, no red/black food, warning sign, daily CBC from day 3. Critical: admit IV fluid monitor. Recovery: convalescent rash white islands red sea; volume overload. Prevention + vaccine. Qs S73 dengue (DHF); S75 DHF; S77 dengue IgM/IgG; S79 dengue serology; S81 plasma leakage; S83 DSS grade III → 5%D/NSS 500 ml in 1–2 h (slide: "NSS loading"); S85 fluid replacement.
Chik: Aedes; acute 1–2 wk high fever, symmetric migratory polyarthralgia small joints, MP rash, conjunctivitis; encephalitis infants/elderly; chronic arthralgia months-years; south, rainy. Ix ↓WBC lymphopenia <1000, ±atypical, ↓plt; <6 d RT-PCR, ≥6 d IgM/IgG. Tx PCM; chronic HCQ/MTX; avoid NSAID while febrile. Qs S90 chik; S92 chik.
IM: EBV, CMV; saliva. Fever, pharyngitis/tonsillitis, ant+post cervical LN, fatigue, palatal petechiae, periorbital edema, HSM, amox rash. PBS lymphocytosis atypical; EBV serology gold: anti-VCA IgM/IgG; anti-EBNA rises late 6–12 wk; Monospot false neg <5 yr. Compl splenic rupture, airway obstruction, AIHA, ↓plt, OHL, lymphoma, NPC. Tx supportive; avoid trauma/contact sport 3 wk; steroid for airway/AIHA/ITP/marked splenomegaly. DDx diphtheria pseudomembrane; GAS doughnut sign? (palatal) only anterior LN no HSM. Qs S100 IM; S102 IM; S104 heterophile antibody; S106 CBC.
Malaria: Pf, Pv, Po, Pm, Pk; Anopheles; Pv & Pf commonest in Thailand; border provinces Kanchanaburi Ratchaburi Chanthaburi Tak Amnat Charoen Narathiwat. Hypnozoite Pv/Po → relapse; recrudescence (incomplete clearance); reinfection. Hx travel within 2 mo; prior malaria in 1 yr; severe (mostly Pf) AOC seizure dyspnea shock jaundice oliguria hemoglobinuria severe anemia. Thick (screen) & thin (species) smear gold; RDT Pf vs non-Pf. Pf normal RBC, double chromatin, multiple rings, accolé, crescent gametocyte; Pm band form; Pv enlarged RBC amoeboid Schüffner; Po enlarged oval fimbriated Schüffner. Uncomplicated Pf: DHA-PPQ + PQ or AS-pyronaridine + PQ; Pv/Po: CQ + PQ; Pm/Pk: CQ (or ACT). PQ kills hypnozoite/gametocyte. Pregnancy Pf 1st trim quinine + clinda; 2–3 trim DHA-PPQ; non-Pf CQ; no PQ. G6PD: reduce/omit PQ. Severe: IV artesunate (quinine if unavailable) → oral ACT + PQ. Prevention: no chemoprophylaxis in Thailand (API <1; recommend when API >10), mosquito avoidance; fever 3 d–2 mo after leaving → suspect. Qs S131 Pv (RBC enlarged); S133 uncomplicated Pf → slide answer "Artesunate + Mefloquine" (old ACT, now DHA-PPQ+PQ); S135 quinine+clinda; S137 hypoglycemia → 50% glucose; S139 IV artesunate (Pk? / Pf with AKI); S141 decreased NADPH (G6PD hemolysis from PQ); S143 repellent.
Anthrax: B. anthracis livestock/wool/meat; cutaneous most common painless eschar edema; inhalation hemorrhagic mediastinitis; GI ulcer; bioterror. Large GPB chains box-car. Cutaneous cipro or doxy; antitoxin severe. Q S148 B. anthracis.
Diphtheria: C. diphtheriae toxin, droplet, incomplete vaccine; grayish-white pseudomembrane tonsil uvula bleeding on scraping; bull neck; laryngeal stridor; myocarditis arrhythmia. Swab GPB club Chinese letter; Elek test toxin. Airway, tracheostomy; DAT; PGS or erythromycin; vaccine DTwP/DTaP <7 y, Td/Tdap ≥7. Qs S153 diphtheria; S155 PGS.
Pertussis: B. pertussis; catarrhal 7–10 d (most contagious), paroxysmal 1–6 wk (20–50 coughs, whoop, post-tussive vomiting; compl pneumonia apnea seizure hernia), convalescent. ↑WBC lymphocytosis; NP culture gold, PCR. Macrolide (azithro, clarith, erythro) or TMP/SMX. Q S161 azithro.
Mucor: Rhizopus, Mucor; spores; DM poor control, immunosupp; rhinocerebral (black eschar palate, proptosis, CN palsy, cavernous sinus thrombosis), pulmonary, cutaneous, GI, disseminated; angioinvasion. Biopsy broad nonseptate ribbon right-angle. Surgery + IV amphotericin B; posaconazole/isavuconazole; control sugar. Q S169 pulmonary mucor (slide answer) [note: neutropenia after chemo classic for aspergillus; slide picture showed broad nonseptate].
Rabies: dog most; flu-like, paresthesia at bite; hydrophobia, hypersalivation, agitation; dx DFA/PCR saliva CSF nuchal skin; palliative. CAT1 lick intact skin; CAT2 nibble/abrasion/scratch no bleeding, ate undercooked rabid meat; CAT3 bite/scratch through skin with bleeding, saliva on mucosa/open wound, bat. Wound: NSS irrigation, delayed primary suture 3–7 d; amox 500 tid 3–5 d prophylaxis; amox/clav 500/125 tid for infection. Animal dead → brain test; alive & all 3 (well kept, ≥2 vaccine doses latest <1 yr, provoked) → observe 10 d; otherwise PEP. Non-vacc: CAT2 vaccine D0,3,7,14,28; CAT3 + RIG. Vaccinated: <6 mo 1 dose D0; >6 mo 2 doses D0,3. Qs S176 rabies; S178 rabies.
Tetanus: C. tetani; trismus, risus, opisthotonus, laryngospasm, autonomic. Airway, IV diazepam, TIG, debridement, metronidazole/PGS, active immunization after recovery. PEP non-vacc: clean 0,1,6 mo; prone + TIG. Vaccinated: clean last >10 y → 1 dose; prone last >5 y → 1 dose. Q S185 tetanus.
CDI: toxin A B; ATB clinda amox amp ceph FQ; PPI, NG, hospitalization. Watery diarrhea; PMC. NAAT/GDH (sens) → toxin EIA (spec). Mild (WBC<15k Cr<1.5) oral vanco/fidaxo (metro oral); severe (WBC≥15k/Cr>1.5/↓BP) oral vanco + IV metro [slide]; FMT recurrent; contact precaution, soap (alcohol doesn't kill spores). Q S191 slide choices—none oral vanco; "IV Vancomycin" vs "oral metronidazole": correct = oral metronidazole (IV vanco ineffective).
Syphilis: early (primary, secondary, early latent <1 y); late (late latent >1 y, tertiary); neuro any stage. Primary painless chancre clean base indurated, painless LN, heals 3–10 wk. Secondary 2–10 wk after: fever LN, MP rash palms soles, condyloma lata, moth-eaten alopecia. Tertiary gumma, CV, tabes. TT (FTA-ABS, TPPA, TPHA, EIA) persist; NTT (RPR VDRL) follow. Early BPG 2.4 MU IM ×1; allergy doxy/TC/azithro/ceftriaxone/erythro; partner 90 d. Late BPG 2.4 MU weekly ×3; allergy doxy/TC. Neuro aqueous PGS IV. Pregnant allergy desensitize. Screen HIV HBV HCV. Qs S207 VDRL; S209 BPG; S211 BPG; S213 BPG single.
Chancroid: H. ducreyi multiple painful ulcers dirty base irregular, painful LN; school of fish GN coccobacilli; ceftriaxone, azithro, cipro, erythro; partner 10 d. LGV: CT L1–L3, painless ulcer, painful bubo, groove sign; NAAT; doxy/azithro/erythro; partner 90 d (CDC 60). Qs S218 chancroid; S220 azithro (penicillin allergy; chancroid).
GC: GN intracellular diplococci; male purulent; female asymptomatic; extragenital; PID epididymitis DGI. Ceftriaxone 1 g IM + azithro/doxy; cefixime 800 + azithro 2 g; gentamicin 160–240 + azithro 2 g; partner 60 d. Q S224 IM ceftriaxone + doxy. NGU: CT, Ureaplasma, M. genitalium, Trichomonas; mucoid discharge; NAAT; doxy or azithro; partner 60 d. Q S229 chlamydial urethritis.
HIV: incubation 2–8 wk, ARS 2–4 wk; chronic 7–10 y; AIDS CD4<200 or AIDS-defining. ARS mono-like rash LN oral ulcer; HIV RNA, p24; 4th gen. Window: RNA 10–12 d, p24 14–16, 4th gen 15–18, 3rd gen 22–24. OI by CD4: <500 pneumococcus, PTB; <200 candida, PCP, miliary/EPTB; <100 crypto, talaromyces, histo, toxo, crypto-sporidium; <50 CMV, MAC. Screen CXR all; CrAg & eye exam if CD4<100. IRIS. No OI → same day ART; OI → treat OI then delay ART. TDF+3TC+DTG or TDF+FTC+DTG. OI prophylaxis CD4<200 or thrush or AIDS-defining (tables image → supplement Thai guideline). PrEP TDF/FTC; PEP TDF/FTC+DTG or TDF/3TC+DTG. SE: TDF nephro Fanconi osteoporosis; AZT BM suppression macrocytic, GI, insulin res, lipoatrophy; ABC HSR HLA-B5701; NVP HSR hepatitis; EFV CNS DLP lipohypertrophy; d4T/ddI insulin res, d4T DLP lipoatrophy; PIs GI insulin res lipohypertrophy. Oral: OHL EBV lateral tongue not scraped no tx; thrush scraped, erythema, clotrimazole troche/nystatin; KOH. S271 EBV; S273 KOH; S275 thrush CD4 250 → topical antifungal + ART (answer image).
Dimorphic: Talaromyces (small pleomorphic, central septum, binary fission), Histoplasma (small oval budding, same size, intracellular), Cryptococcus (large round budding capsule). S281 intracapsular → cryptococcosis; S283 central necrosis papule → talaromycosis; S285 ? (penicillosis/histo).
