## Key facts
- Obstructive: TLC↑ RV↑ FEV1↓↓↓ FVC↓ ratio <0.7 ; Restrictive: TLC↓ RV↓ FEV1 N/↓ FVC↓ ratio N/↑ (6). Post-BD ↑FEV1 ≥12% และ ≥200 ml = reversible → asthma; no → COPD (7)
- COPD: smoking/pollution, chronic productive cough, barrel chest, prolonged expiration, end-exp wheeze, rhonchi (8). Ix: FEV1/FVC<0.7 post-BD, ↑FEV1<12%; ABG ถ้า SpO2<92% หรือ AECOPD; CXR flattened diaphragm, horizontal ribs, ↓lung marking; CBC ↑Hct (9). Dx ต้องมีทุกข้อ: clinical+risk + post-BD FEV1/FVC<0.7 (11)
- AECOPD: O2 keep SpO2 88–92%; NIPPV ถ้า PaCO2 ≥45 & pH ≤7.35, persistent hypoxemia, severe dyspnea, accessory muscles; ETT ถ้า NIPPV fail (12). NB SABA หรือ SABA/SAMA; systemic steroid pred PO หรือ MP/dexa IV (13). ATB (amox/clav, azithro, doxy) เฉพาะ ↑sputum purulence + ↑dyspnea หรือ ↑cough, หรือ on MV; ส่วนใหญ่ virus (14)
- COPD long-term: step1 LABA หรือ LAMA; step2 LABA+LAMA; step3 +ICS ถ้า eos ≥300 (16). GOLD A: a bronchodilator; B: LABA+LAMA; E: LABA+LAMA (+ICS ถ้า eos >300). LABA salmeterol/formoterol, LAMA tiotropium (17). Smoking cessation, vaccine pneumococcal/influenza, NAC, LTOT ถ้า PaO2 ≤55 หรือ SaO2 ≤88% at rest (18). Prevention exacerbation: smoking cessation, vaccines, compliance, pulmonary rehab (28)
- Old Qs COPD: 19 (smoker wheeze rhonchi → COPD), 21 (insp+exp wheeze at sternum → tracheal tumor), 23–24 (COPD exacerbation due to RLL pneumonia), 25–26 (prevent exacerbation: LABA+ICS among choices; ปัจจุบัน LABA+LAMA), 27–28 (influenza vaccine)
- Asthma: atopy; trigger exercise/cold/allergen/smoke; dry cough nocturnal; exp wheeze; AR/eczema (29). Predicted PEF = Ht(cm)x5 −400 L/min (30). Dx ทุกข้อ: clinical + FEV1/FVC<0.7 + variability ≥1: post-BD ↑FEV1 ≥12% & ≥200 ml; post-tx 4 wk ↑FEV1 ≥12% & ≥200; methacholine ↓FEV1 ≥20%; PEF: variability 2 wk >10%, post-BD ↑PEF ≥20%, post-tx 4 wk ↑PEF ≥20% (31). Allergy: specific IgE, skin prick (33)
- Asthma exac: mild–mod O2, NB salbutamol, pred PO (35); severe: O2, ETT if resp failure, SABA+SAMA, pred PO/MP IV/dexa IV, ± IV MgSO4/high-dose ICS (36)
- Asthma long-term (GINA track 1): step1–2 sx <4–5 d/wk → PRN low-dose ICS/formoterol; step3 sx most days or waking ≥1/wk → daily low-dose ICS/form; step4 daily sx or waking ≥1/wk + low lung function → medium ICS/form; step5 → high ICS/form + add-on LAMA (38–40). Avoid trigger, vaccines, F/U spirometry (41)
- Old Qs asthma: 42 asthma; 44 & 46 PFT; 50 NB salbutamol; 52 pH 7.1 PaCO2 70 → ETT; 54 stridor+wheeze not responding SABA → epinephrine NB; 56 add dexamethasone IV; 58 salbutamol NB + oral pred; 60 admit; 62 observe 1 hr; 64 add inhaled LABA (ICS/formoterol); 67 RUL atelectasis → suction
- Bronchiectasis: recurrent infection → permanent dilation; causes infection, CF, ABPA, COPD, immunodef, autoimmune; copious mucopurulent sputum, hemoptysis (69). CXR insensitive; HRCT tram-track, signet-ring, honeycomb, saccular/cystic (70). Acute: O2, mucolytics, airway clearance, ATB amox/clav or cipro (72). Long-term: stop smoking, airway clearance, vaccines, mucolytic, BD, tx cause, azithro/erythro ≥3 mo (73). Old Q 74 infected bronchiectasis; 76 honeycomb → bronchiectasis
- Pneumothorax: PSP apical blebs, tall thin men; SSP COPD/TB/PCP; traumatic (78). Sudden pleuritic pain, dyspnea, SC emphysema, ↓expansion ↓fremitus, hyperresonance, ↓BS; CXR visceral pleural line (79). Tension: ↓BP ↑PR trachea shift contralateral, JVP↑; needle decompression 4–5th ICS anterior to MAL then ICD; dx clinical (80). Mx: asymptomatic conservative; symptomatic high-risk → admit+ICD; high risk = hemodynamic compromise, significant hypoxemia, bilateral, underlying lung dz, age ≥50 + smoking, hemopneumothorax; size not indication (84). Symptomatic non-high risk: conservative / ambulatory device / needle aspiration; fail → ICD; persistent air leak >5–7 d → bigger ICD, pleurodesis, EBV, surgery (85). Surgery for recurrence prevention: high-risk occupations, tension, 2nd ipsilateral/1st contralateral (86). RPE: unilateral, <24 h after rapid re-expansion; prevent drain <1–1.5 L, avoid pleural pressure < −20 cmH2O (87). Old Q 88 tension → ICD; 90 re-expansion edema
- PE: DVT; immobilization, hypercoag, OCP, pregnancy, cancer; sudden dyspnea, pleuritic pain, hemoptysis, syncope, ↓BP large, loud P2, clear lungs, ± DVT (92). ECG sinus tachy, RBBB, S1Q3T3 (93). CXR normal, effusion, Hampton hump, Westermark; ABG PaO2<80, resp alkalosis; ↑trop ↑BNP ↑D-dimer (94). CTPA confirm; compression US; echo if unstable: RV dilation, D-shape, RV hypokinesia (95). Simple Wells ≥2 likely (96). Algorithm: unlikely → D-dimer R/O; likely no shock → (LMWH) CTPA; shock → echo RV dysfunction → rt-PA, IV fluid, NE, IV heparin (97). Initial: unstable SBP<90 resus, IV fluid <500 mL, vasopressor; empirical anticoag: unstable IV heparin, stable SC LMWH (98). Unstable: rt-PA (alteplase) or embolectomy if C/I; stable low bleed: heparin/LMWH/fondaparinux; high bleed: IVC filter; long-term 3–6 mo DOAC/warfarin/LMWH, extend e.g. active cancer; heparin if GFR<30 or severe obesity (99). Old Qs: 100 PE; 102 enoxaparin; 104 duplex US; 106 LMWH; 108 CTPA; 110 CTPA; 112 CTA; 114 heparin
- CAP typical: S.pneumo, H.flu, M.cat; acute high fever productive cough, crepitation, bronchial BS, ↑fremitus, egophony; CXR alveolar (lobar/broncho) (116). Atypical: Mycoplasma, Chlamydia, Legionella, virus; slow, low-grade fever, dry cough, extrapulmonary sx; CXR perihilar interstitial; Mycoplasma rash, EM, hemolytic anemia, cold agglutinin (117). Risk pathogens: alcohol S.pn/H.flu; COPD S.pn/H.flu/M.cat; aspiration anaerobes; IVDU/skin S.aureus; post-flu S.pn/S.aureus; bullous myringitis Mycoplasma; relative bradycardia Legionella (118)
- CURB-65: 0–1 OPD, ≥2 admit, ≥3 ICU; confusion, BUN>20, RR≥30, SBP≤90/DBP≤60, age≥65 (124). ATB 5–7 d (125). OPD no U/D: amox 1 g tid, doxy 100 bid, azithro 500 then 250, clarithro 500 bid (127). - Pleural effusion: transudate (HF, cirrhosis, nephrotic, PD) vs exudate (parapneumonic, malignancy, TB, PE) (160). PE: trachea shift contralateral if massive, ↓expansion ↓fremitus dull ↓BS (161). WBC >10,000 parapneumonic/empyema/autoimmune/PE; RBC >5,000 hemothorax/malignant; glucose <60 malignant/parapneumonic/empyema/TB/autoimmune (170). pH <7.2 complicated/empyema/malignant; ADA >40 TB; amylase >200 pancreatitis/esophageal perforation; TG >110 chylothorax (171). Parapneumonic table: simple <½ hemithorax free-flow pH>7.2 glu>60 C/S neg → ATB observe; complicated >½, loculated, pH<7.2, glu<60 → ATB + drain; empyema frank pus or G/S+/C/S+ → ATB + drain (172). - Lung abscess: aspiration, bronchial obstruction, hematogenous; anaerobes; risk impaired consciousness/alcohol, swallow, dental caries, immunocompromised (197).
