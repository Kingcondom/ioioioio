## Key facts
GERD: heartburn, regurg, dysphagia/odynophagia; triggers lying after meal, fat, caffeine, alcohol, soda; extraesophageal: chronic dry/night cough, hoarseness, dental erosion. EGD if alarm (dysphagia, odynophagia, GI bleed, anemia, wt loss, vomit >10/d) or failed med. Tx PPI 4–8 wk: omeprazole 20–40 OD, esomeprazole 20–40 OD, pantoprazole 40 OD; LSM (avoid late meals/trigger food, wt loss, elevate head of bed, stop smoking/alcohol/caffeine). S7 algorithm: alarm→EGD; typical: LSM+standard PPI 4–8 wk → no response → double dose/switch 8–12 wk → refractory; NCCP: LSM+double dose PPI 8 wk; extraesophageal: exclude other → if both extra+typical → double PPI 8–12 wk; response→ stop/step down; recurrent → on-demand/continuous.
Dyspepsia: EGD if onset ≥50, failed med, alarm (GI bleed, odyno/dysphagia, vomit>10/d, wt loss, FHx gastric/esoph CA 1st degree, epigastric mass, IDA). S11: uninvestigated → ≥50 or alarm → EGD+HP testing (abnormal→tx cause; normal→functional dyspepsia: PPI, prokinetic, TCA, cytoprotective); No → PPI±prokinetic 4–8 wk → HP test & treat; no response → EGD.
HP: 1st line triple (amox 1g bid + clarith 500 bid + PPI bid 14 d; resistance rising), sequential (amox 1g bid 5d → metro 500 + clarith 500 bid 5d; PPI bid 10d), concomitant (amox 1g bid + metro 400 tid + clarith 500 bid + PPI bid 10d); 2nd line levo-amox triple (levo 500 OD + amox 1 g bid + PPI bid 10d), bismuth quadruple (bismuth subsalicylate 524 qid + metro 250 qid + tetracycline 500 qid + PPI bid 14d). Confirm eradication ≥4 wk after tx by UBT or stool Ag.
Slide Qs: S15 56M new onset → gastroscopy; S17 70F → EGD; S19 55M wt loss → gastroscopy; S21 20M → PPI; S23 → UBT in 4 wk.

UGIB causes: PU, varices, erosive gastritis, esophagitis, MW tear, malignancy. Table S26 (MW: retching before; esoph ulcer: GERD odynophagia; PU: pain NSAID ASA; stress gastritis ICU; varices CLD HBV alcohol; malig cachexia; aortoenteric fistula: aortic aneurysm repair). Scores: Rockall (mortality; age <60/60–79/≥80; shock; comorbidity; dx; SRH), GBS (score 0 → no admit, elective EGD; ≥1 admit+EGD), AIMS65. Variceal: painless, usually hematemesis, V/S change >90%, portal HT signs. Initial: ABC, ETT if AOC or severe hematemesis; LRS/NSS; PRC keep Hb ≥7 (≥8 CVD); correct coagulopathy keep INR <2.5, Plt ≥50,000; NG only if source unclear (acute severe hematochezia R/O UGIB). Non-variceal: PPI 80 mg IV bolus then 8 mg/h (omeprazole/pantoprazole/esomeprazole). Variceal: somatostatin 250 mcg bolus→250 mcg/h; octreotide 50 mcg→50 mcg/h; terlipressin 1 mg IV q8h (? slide); ATB prophylaxis ceftriaxone 1 g IV OD 7 d or norfloxacin 400 bid 7 d; SB tube if still bleeding after vasoactive 1–2 h while waiting EGD. Specific: EGD dx+tx; PU → PPI + HP test; MW → PPI; EV → vasoactive + EVL/ES; TIPS if failed; gastric varix → vasoactive + ES. Post-EGD: non-variceal PPI IV/oral to 72 h, HP eradicate; variceal vasoactive 3–5 d; secondary prophylaxis propranolol + EVL.
Slide Qs: S37 ETT; S39 PRC group O; S41 retain NG; S43 no transfusion; S45 FFP; S47 variceal; S49 somatostatin; S51 IV somatostatin; S53 beta blocker (secondary prophylaxis propranolol+EVL).

Typhoid: S. Typhi/Paratyphi, fecal-oral; macrophages→LN, liver, spleen, BM, Peyer patch distal ileum, GB. Fever, constipation early then nonbloody diarrhea, relative bradycardia, rose spots, HSM; complication wk 2–4 bleeding/perforation. Ix hemoculture, stool C/S; Widal not recommended. Tx ceftriaxone, azithro, TMP/SMX, FQ (resistance rising). Chronic carrier: stool + 12 mo after; GB colonization; U/S GB; cipro or TMP/SMX 2–4 wk; cholecystectomy if gallstone; ↑GB cancer. S60 → S. Typhi; S62 food handler → U/S liver & GB.
Food poisoning: preformed toxin; vomiting predominant (C. perfringens diarrhea); same food same symptoms; supportive. S. aureus (food left room temp), B. cereus (fried rice, meat, veg), C. perfringens (canned food, meat) [slide mapping].
Infectious GE: watery – virus (noro, rota, adeno), ETEC, C. diff, C. perfringens, V. cholerae, Giardia, Cryptosporidium; bloody – EIEC, EHEC, Campylobacter, NTS, Shigella, Yersinia, V. parahaemolyticus, V. vulnificus, E. histolytica. Clues: ETEC travel; cholera seafood/water profuse; C diff ATB; crypto immunocompromised; Campylobacter poultry; vibrio para/vulnificus shellfish; NTS poultry/eggs. Stool exam/culture if severe dehydration, >1 wk, high fever/bloody, elderly/immunocompromised. Mx: mild watery ORS; severe watery or bloody: ORS/IV, ATB cipro/levo/azithro 3 d; antidiarrheal bismuth, racecadotril, loperamide (not in inflammatory diarrhea → toxic megacolon). S69 IV fluid; S71 V. cholera; S73 cholera; S75 Campylobacter.
IBS: women>men, 20–39 yr; psych/fibromyalgia; pain related to defecation; triggers stress/meals; PE normal. Rome IV: recurrent pain ≥1 d/wk in last 3 mo + ≥2 (related to defecation, change frequency, change form); onset ≥6 mo. Alarm → colonoscopy; calprotectin/CRP if suspect IBD; stool culture. Mx: low FODMAP, high fiber low fat, exercise; antispasmodic (buscopan, dicyclomine); antidiarrheal (loperamide, ondansetron); laxative (PEG, MOM, lactulose). S82 algorithm (ไทย): pain→antispasmodic/peppermint oil → TCA; constipation → fiber, osmotic/stimulant → secretagogue/5-HT4/IBAT; diarrhea → loperamide/ondansetron → 5-HT3 antagonist, rifaximin, bile acid sequestrant, eluxadoline → TCA; refractory → GI. S83 IBS; S85 IBS; S87 high fibre diet.

AP: gallstone 40–70%, alcohol 25–35%, hyperTG, hyperCa, drugs, ERCP, malignancy, infection. Pain radiates back, better bending forward, N/V, fever, decreased BS, jaundice (cholangitis), crepitation/effusion, Grey Turner (flank), Cullen (periumbilical). Dx ≥2/3: pain, lipase/amylase ≥3xUNL (lipase better), imaging. AXR: colon cut-off, sentinel loop – not for dx. U/S: enlarged hypoechoic pancreas, fluid; GS/sludge/biliary dilatation. CT if uncertain dx, not improve 48–72 h, severe after 5–7 d (necrosis). Severity: revised Atlanta (mild no OF; mod severe transient OF <48 h or local complications; severe persistent OF >48 h), Marshall, BISAP, APACHE II, Ranson, SIRS. Mild: LRS, morphine, early enteral low fat. Mod–severe: + NG/NJ feeding, CT 5–7 d, carbapenem if necrosis >30%/OF/not tolerate feed (slide), ERCP if GS pancreatitis with cholangitis. Cholecystectomy timing: mild this admission or <2 wk; moderate after 3 wk; severe after 6 wk. Infected necrosis: extraluminal gas, FNA, persistent fever, shock, high scores, ↓Hb ↑WBC; tx carbapenem, drainage if failed. Slide Qs: S99 AP; S101 US upper abd; S103 LRS/morphine/early enteral; S105 NG (early enteral/NG/NJ); S107 low fat diet; S109 IV fluid analgesia; S111 SIRS; S113 operate this admission; S115 infected pancreatitis.
CP: alcohol most common, ductal obstruction, smoking, SLE, hyperTG; pain (worse after eating); exocrine insuff → steatorrhea, malabsorption, ADEK deficiency; endocrine → DM. CT/MRCP calcification, duct dilatation/stricture; AXR calcification; amylase/lipase normal; pancreatic function test. Mx stop alcohol/smoking, enzyme replacement, NSAID/opioid, tx cause, vitamins/antidiabetic. S120 → vit D deficiency (↓Ca ↓PO4 ↑ALP ↑PTH).

LFT S122: hepatocellular (AST/ALT ↑–↑↑↑), cholestatic (ALP/GGT/bili ↑), isolated hyperbili, cirrhosis (alb↓ INR↑). S123: AST:ALT >2 alcohol; >1000 viral/drug/ischemic; ALP+GGT biliary; ALP normal GGT bone; unconjugated: hemolysis, Gilbert, Crigler-Najjar. S124 chart: normal <30–40; chronic viral/steato <~300; alcoholic <~600 AST; drug/acute viral/ischemic/APAP to >1000.
Acute hepatitis causes: viral A–E, CMV, EBV, alcohol, drug, ischemic, AIH, Wilson. Chronic: HBV, HCV, MASLD/MASH, ALD, AIH, drug, Wilson, hemochromatosis. Prodrome flu-like then jaundice/RUQ 1–2 wk; tender hepatomegaly; ALT>AST. Profile: HAV IgM; HBsAg + anti-HBc IgM; anti-HCV + HCV RNA; anti-HEV IgM. Table S129. If not stated IgM → IgG/total.
HAV: no chronic; supportive no F/U; IgG immune. HBV: F/U AST/ALT/HBsAg/anti-HBs 6 mo; chronic = HBsAg >6 mo; antiviral if decompensated (TB>2, ascites, HE), compensated cirrhosis + detectable DNA, DNA ≥2000 + (ALT >1.5xULN, APRI >0.5 or FIB-4 >1.45, age >35). TAF, TDF, ETV. No antiviral: F/U AST ALT plt q6–12 mo; ALT>1.5x → DNA. Co-infection HIV/HCV. HCC surveillance U/S+AFP q6–12 mo if cirrhosis, men >40 / women >50, FHx HCC. HBV can → HCC without cirrhosis. Serology table S137. HCV: 90% asymptomatic; acute: anti-HCV neg + RNA pos; mostly chronic; chronic: anti-HCV + → RNA +; SOF/VEL (± ribavirin if cirrhosis); HCC surveillance if cirrhosis/advanced fibrosis; resolved = non-immune. Vaccines HAV, HBV; none for HCV.
Slide Qs: S144 hep A; S146 HAV not cirrhosis; S148 anti-HAV IgM; S150–153 hep A supportive; S154 acute HBV; S156 F/U 3–6 mo; S158 HBsAg anti-HBs; S160 chronic HBV normal ALT F/U; S162 chronic HBV; S164 HBV DNA; S166 antiviral.

APAP: therapeutic ≤4 g/d; intentional single >7–10 g; misadventure 10–20 g in 3 d with risk (alcohol, malnutrition, CYP2E1 inducers: phenobarb, phenytoin, INH, zidovudine). Metabolism: 85–90% glucuronide/sulfate; 5–9% CYP2E1 → NAPQI, glutathione; <5% unchanged. Stages I 0.5–24 h; II 24–48; III 72–96 hepatic necrosis; IV 4–14 d recovery. Mx: gastric lavage <1 h, AC within 4 h; NAC repletes glutathione, start within 8 h; indication level above treatment line or single dose >150 mg/kg or 7.5 g; level at ≥4 h; Rumack-Matthew (150 mg/L at 4 h treatment line). S174 APAP toxicity; S176 acetaminophen (slide).
Ischemic: HF, shock, resp failure; AST/ALT >1000, ↑TB, ↑BUN Cr; tx hemodynamic. S179, S181 ischemic.
ALD: steatosis (reversible); steatohepatitis: jaundice fever, tender HM, AST/ALT >2 (AST usually <300), ↑GGT; stop alcohol, nutrition, steroid if severe; cirrhosis irreversible, transplant. GGT falls when stop. S186 ALD; S188, 190, 192, 194 alcoholic hepatitis; S196 GGT.
Fatty liver: MASLD (old NAFLD), MASH (old NASH); risk obesity DM DLP TG; ALT>AST; U/S ↑echo; low fat diet, wt loss, DM/DLP control. S200 fatty liver; S202 NASH.
Wilson: AR; liver, brain (dysarthria, gait, parkinsonism, tremor, dementia, hallucination), KF ring; AST>ALT; ↓ceruloplasmin (<20 mg/dL S230), ↓total Cu, ↑free Cu, ↑24h urine Cu (>100 mcg/24h); tx Cu restriction, chelator, zinc. S205 Wilson.

PLA: 50–70 yr; DM cirrhosis cancer; K. pneumoniae E. coli, GPC, anaerobes; biliary most common; RUQ pain fever, Rt shoulder pain. CT rim enhancement; aspiration. Tx drainage + IV ATB 4–6 wk (ceftriaxone+metronidazole, amp/sulbactam, pip/tazo); surgery if large/ruptured/peritonitis/underlying. S216 PLA.
Amebiasis: E. histolytica; diarrhea/dysentery tenesmus; stool cyst/trophozoite with RBC; PCR/Ag; metronidazole then paromomycin. ALA: Hx diarrhea; solitary round hypoechoic; aspiration R/O PLA; serology can't distinguish past; PO metronidazole 7–10 d then paromomycin; drainage if >5 cm, left lobe, not improved 5–7 d. S218 8 cm → percutaneous drainage; S220 3 cm → oral metronidazole.
Melioidosis: B. pseudomallei; Isan, DM, CKD, thalassemia; LUQ pain splenic abscess; melioid titer; bipolar GNB; Swiss cheese liver & spleen; ATB alone ceftazidime, meropenem. S222 ceftazidime.

Cirrhosis: stellate (Ito) cells fibrosis; causes ALD, HBV, HCV, fatty, AIH, Wilson, hemochromatosis. Signs portal HT (ascites, splenomegaly, caput) & CLD (parotid, testicular atrophy, gynecomastia, spider, clubbing, Terry nail, palmar erythema). LFT AST>ALT, ↓alb ↑glob, ↑PT/INR, ↓Hb ↓plt; U/S nodular, small, left lobe hypertrophy; biopsy gold. FIB-4 (age, AST, ALT, plt), APRI, Fibrotest, elastography. W/U cause: HBsAg, anti-HCV, lipid/HbA1c, iron studies, ceruloplasmin/copper; S228–233 table: ALD AST/ALT>2 & <500; hemochromatosis ferritin >200 F/ >300 M, TSAT >45%; AIH IgG ANA SMA anti-LKM1; PBC AMA; PSC MRCP beaded. Complications: ascites, EV, SBP, HE, HRS, HCC; decompensated = ascites, variceal hemorrhage, HE, jaundice. Mx: tx cause, avoid hepatotoxin, nutrition, vaccines HAV HBV influenza pneumococcal; EGD surveillance (no varix q2–3 yr, small q1–2 yr, large >5 mm/red wale → propranolol); HCC U/S+AFP q6mo; transplant if refractory ascites/HE/variceal hemorrhage, MELD ≥15; skip EGD if TE <20 kPa + plt >150,000. S238 stellate; S240 decompensated; S242 HBsAg anti-HCV; S244 EGD.
Ascites: cirrhosis 81–84%; high SAAG portal HT mechanism; low SAAG hypoalbumin, malignancy, infection. Shifting dullness, fluid thrill. Paracentesis indications: new ascites, cirrhotic ascites admitted, ascites + new fever/pain/worsening/LFT deterioration/AKI/HE/leukocytosis. Basic: cell count, albumin, protein, sugar, LDH, G/S, C/S; cytology; ADA. SAAG ≥1.1 high: protein <2.5 cirrhosis/late Budd-Chiari; ≥2.5 RHF, early BC, pericarditis; low SAAG: protein <2.5 nephrotic; ≥2.5 peritoneal TB/CA, pancreatitis. Mx: mild no tx; moderate Na restriction + spironolactone (1st) ± furosemide; severe add therapeutic paracentesis (albumin 6–8 g/L if >5 L); refractory TIPS, transplant. S260 pancreatitis; S262 cirrhosis; S264 high salt intake; S266 paracentesis.
SBP: translocation; E. coli, Klebsiella, strep; monomicrobial; risk UGIB, prior SBP, ascitic protein <1.5; fever, pain, ↑ascites, HE. PMN ≥250 + ≥2 organisms → secondary (CT, surgery); PMN ≥250 + 1 organism → SBP; PMN ≥250 culture neg → CNNA (tx as SBP); PMN <250 + culture + → bacterascites (symptomatic tx, else observe). Tx ATB ≥5–7 d ceftriaxone, cefotaxime, IV cipro; albumin if TB >4 or Cr >1 or BUN >30. Prophylaxis: cirrhosis+UGIB; low protein <1.5 + renal/liver failure; prior SBP; ceftriaxone, norfloxacin, TMP/SMX. S268 CNNA; S270 cefotaxime; S272 no answer shown on slide (options odd).
HE: BIG SCALP (blood transfusion, infection, GI bleed, sedative, constipation, AKI, low K, protein). NH3. Cognitive/consciousness/asterixis; West Haven MHE, I–IV (S275). Dx by exclusion (S276 ddx list). Tx precipitant; lactulose 1st (2–3 stools/d); rifaximin; prevention lactulose ± rifaximin. S278 HE + SBP → paracentesis, lactulose + tx SBP; S280 paracentesis; S282 paracentesis; S284 lactulose.
