## Outline (2–3)
SLE · APS · RA · Seronegative SpA (AS, ReA, PsA) · Crystal (gout & hyperuricemia, CPPD) · Septic arthritis · OA · IIM · IgA vasculitis (HSP)

## General (4–5)
- Articular vs periarticular (4): location joint line diffuse vs focal; inflammation diffuse vs focal; pain on movement active+passive vs active ROM some direction
- Synovial fluid (5 = 80 = 108): Normal transparent clear WBC<200 PMN<25% · Non-inflam transparent yellow <2,000 <25% (OA trauma viral drug) · Inflammatory cloudy yellow 2,000–50,000 >50% crystal yes (crystal, RA, SLE, SpA) · Septic cloudy yellow >50,000 >90% C/S + (bacterial) · Trauma cloudy red (fracture, ligament injury, hemophilia)
